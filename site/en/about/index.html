About

Read beyond the signal, into the structure.

SIGNA ET STRUCTURA is a market research publication that treats public information, price, volume, liquidity, social activity, on-chain data and historical cases as evidence.

Editorial principle

Signal → Structure → Evidence. We move from observable signals to hypotheses about underlying structure, then test those hypotheses against evidence.

What we publish

Market Watch, Theme Watch, SNS Trends, Historical Case Studies, Protocol / Asset Research, Methods, Data / Charts and Research Notes.

What we do not publish

Unreviewed internal research, confidential information, unsupported claims, guaranteed-return language, individualized trading instructions or content that could facilitate market manipulation.

Published by Smile Company LLC.
