SPCXc/USDC liquidity fragments as a 5% fee pool forms on Uniswap V4

A new 5% fee SPCXc/USDC pool is visible on Uniswap V4 on Base alongside the existing Aerodrome market. At observation time, the Uniswap V4 pool showed $18,860.87 of liquidity, $25,898.56 of 24-hour volume, 284 transactions and an age of roughly six hours. On the same GeckoTerminal surface, the related Aerodrome SPCXc/USDC market showed roughly $11,420 of liquidity and $380,960 of 24-hour volume, materially below the $44,954.13 liquidity recorded for Aerodrome at 03:43 JST on 2026-09-05.

Market Watch

Aerodrome METAc/USDC Display APY Falls to 582% as the AERO Reward Spike Normalizes

On Base, DeFi Terminal now shows Aerodrome Slipstream USDC-METAc at 581.51% APY, composed of 79.29% base yield and 502.22% reward yield, with roughly $857.6K in TVL. That is 1,256.49 percentage points below the 1,838.00% reading observed on the same surface on 2026-09-04 at 11:26 JST, with most of the move coming from the AERO reward component. The current reading is close to the page's 30-day average of 594.14%.

Market Watch

Base Expands Tokenized Stocks to Ten as a Second Wave of Aerodrome USDC Pools Forms

Base's official Coinbase Tokenized Stocks list has expanded from the original NVDAc, METAc, AAPLc and GOOGLc cohort to ten names, adding AMZNc, MSFTc, MSTRc, SNDKc, SPCXc and TSLAc. This observation also found live Aerodrome Slipstream 3 USDC pools with trading activity for AMZNc, MSTRc and SPCXc. The new pools remain thin, and no reliable public APY or independently confirmed AERO, Merkl or Beefy incentive for the second-wave names was identified at the observation time.

AERO-Driven APY Re-Expands in Aerodrome's METAc/USDC Pool

DefiLlama's METAc RWA dashboard now shows the Aerodrome Slipstream USDC-METAc pool on Base at 2,696.82% APY and $709,974 TVL.
