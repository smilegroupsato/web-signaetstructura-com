Market Watch

Meta-backed USDC rates on Morpho rise toward 17% while three peer stock markets sit near 5%

Market-level pricing has become visible for Morpho's USDC markets backed by Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base. Apple, Alphabet and Nvidia were reported near 90% utilization with roughly 5.06% lender APY and 5.62% borrower APY, while Meta reached about 97% utilization with 16.98% lender APY and 17.52% borrower APY. No new external incentive was identified; lender yield is funded by USDC interest paid by borrowers.

Morpho activates variable- and fixed-rate loans against Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base

Morpho says stock-backed loans are now live on Base using Coinbase Tokenized Stocks as collateral. Users can lend and borrow at variable and fixed rates, while individual curators set market parameters and compliance controls. DefiLlama's AAPLc dashboard currently attributes $45,097 of DeFi Active TVL to Morpho Blue.

Market Watch

Beefy USDC-SPCXc displayed APY re-expands to 324% as TVL contracts

On Base, Portals shows Beefy Moo USDC-SPCXc rising from 235.84% APY at 13:08 JST on 2026-09-17 to 323.55% at 17:55 JST, while TVL fell from about $152.9K to $127.7K. That is an 87.71 percentage-point APY increase, or about 37.2%, alongside a roughly $25.2K, or 16.5%, TVL decline. The associated Beefy CLM SPCXc-USDC Reward Pool also moved in the same direction, reaching 159.62% APY with $149.43K TVL.
