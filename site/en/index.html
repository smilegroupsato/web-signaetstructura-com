Market Watch

Beefy USDC-SPCXc displayed APY re-expands to roughly 193%-235% across data surfaces

Displayed yield on Beefy's Base USDC-SPCXc strategy has re-expanded since the prior observation. The Portals direct page shows 192.76% APY with $152.11K TVL, DefiLlama shows 202.80% with $144.24K, and DeFi Terminal shows 234.80% with $153.1K. The related Beefy CLM Reward Pool also reads 118.87% on Portals and 122.60% on DefiLlama, indicating that the move is not confined to a single data surface, although Reward Pool TVL differs materially between providers.

Market Watch

Spectra brings PT/YT fixed-term rate markets to Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base

Spectra is offering fixed-maturity markets on Base for AAPLc, NVDAc, METAc and GOOGLc that split yield generated by TAU Stock Carry Vaults into Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT). On Portals' dedicated market pages, the AAPLc, NVDAc and METAc markets maturing on 2026-09-30 show roughly $4.77K-$5.75K of TVL and displayed APYs of 12.48%-15.86%.

Market Watch

TAU Stock Carry Vaults activate Morpho/Steakhouse carry on Base

TAU Labs announced that its Stock Carry Vaults on Base have activated carry strategies that borrow USDC against Coinbase Tokenized Stocks through Morpho markets curated by SteakhouseFi. DefiLlama currently shows AAPLCT at $38,186 TVL and 10.27% APY, split into 0.27% base and 10.00% reward APY.

Market Watch

Beefy USDC-MSFTc direct-page APY falls to 91.86% as provider readings converge within about 0.5 percentage points

On Base, Beefy's Moo USDC-MSFTc displayed APY on the Portals direct page fell from 141.68% at the 2026-09-16 16:22 JST observation to 91.86% at 19:02 JST, a 49.82 percentage-point decline. The current reading is now close to Portals' 91.47% list view and DeFi Terminal's 91.94%, narrowing the same-market display range to about 0.47 percentage points. TVL remains near $170,000 rather than showing a comparable capital move.
