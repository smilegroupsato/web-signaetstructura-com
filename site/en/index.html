Market Watch

Zyfai reports more than $225K of tokenized-stock deposits in one week

Zyfai says more than $225K of Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base were deposited through its platform in one week. Its displayed yields are up to 38.02% APY for NVDAc and 20.47% for AAPLc, GOOGLc and METAc, including incentives. The same public post says the additional USDC incentive for tokenized-stock depositors has about one week remaining.

Market Watch

Bitwise Mag7X crosses $1M as Merkl-eligible TVL rises about 94% from September 4

Bitwise Mag7X Automated Token Portfolio on Base has moved above the $1 million scale. Glider reported $1,020,271 across 1,585 users as of September 18, while the current Merkl opportunity shows a 10% APR, $983.64K of eligible TVL and 269.558 USDC of daily rewards. The September 4 public observation recorded $507.76K of Merkl-eligible TVL and 139.147 USDC per day, implying roughly 93.7% growth in both eligible TVL and daily rewards while the incentive rate itself remains unchanged at 10%.

Market Watch

superNVDA TVL tops $400K as one-day inflow nears $80K and APY eases to 17.08%

Superform's NVDA SuperVault (superNVDA) on Base is showing $400.44K of TVL, a one-day flow of +$79,630.62 and a headline APY of 17.08% on Portals. In the public observation on 2026-09-15, Portals showed $305.4K of TVL and 19.84% APY, implying TVL has risen about 31.1% while the headline APY has fallen 2.76 percentage points. Capital continues to enter the vault as displayed yield compresses.

Market Watch

Meta-backed USDC rates on Morpho rise toward 17% while three peer stock markets sit near 5%

Market-level pricing has become visible for Morpho's USDC markets backed by Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base. Apple, Alphabet and Nvidia were reported near 90% utilization with roughly 5.06% lender APY and 5.62% borrower APY, while Meta reached about 97% utilization with 16.98% lender APY and 17.52% borrower APY. No new external incentive was identified; lender yield is funded by USDC interest paid by borrowers.
