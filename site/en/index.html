Market Watch

New DiamondPepe/NVDAc market on Base extends tokenized stocks as quote assets

DiamondPepe/NVDAc is active as a new Uniswap V4 market on Base. GeckoTerminal showed roughly $47.6K-$58.5K liquidity and $2.02M-$2.03M of 24-hour volume, while DEX Screener showed about $47K liquidity but only $165K of volume. Both confirm the market, but the large provider discrepancy means headline turnover is not a settled figure.

Market Watch

A GOOGLc-quoted meme market gains real liquidity on Base

RAWR trades on Base against GOOGLc, Coinbase's tokenized Alphabet stock, using the equity token as the quote asset. RAWR's official site makes the GOOGLc denomination central to the market design. Market aggregators showed roughly $140,000 of liquidity and $400,000-$600,000+ of 24-hour volume, extending tokenized stocks into crypto-native price discovery.

Market Watch

LONG Uses Tokenized Stocks as Quote and Fee-Settlement Assets for Creator Key Markets on Base

LONG on Base lets Creator Keys tied to verified identities trade against tokenized stocks as quote assets. Its docs list 13 B20 stock tokens, including AAPLc, NVDAc and SPCXc. GeckoTerminal showed a kGOLONGFUN/NVDAc Uniswap v4 pool with about $17,009 liquidity and $654 of 24-hour volume, extending tokenized stocks into creator-market pricing and fee settlement.

Market Watch

Stockify converts trading fees into Base B20 equities and distributes them directly to holders

Stockify converts STFY trading fees into Coinbase B20 tokenized equities and distributes them to eligible holders. Its ETH/STFY Uniswap v4 market charges a 3% hook fee, with 90% directed to equity acquisition. The active index was 25% each NVDAc, AAPLc, GOOGLc and METAc; the distribution desk showed 317 settled cycles and $69,736.08 distributed.
