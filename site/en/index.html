Market Watch

Stockify converts trading fees into Base B20 equities and distributes them directly to holders

Stockify converts STFY trading fees into Coinbase B20 tokenized equities and distributes them to eligible holders. Its ETH/STFY Uniswap v4 market charges a 3% hook fee, with 90% directed to equity acquisition. The active index was 25% each NVDAc, AAPLc, GOOGLc and METAc; the distribution desk showed 317 settled cycles and $69,736.08 distributed.

A Fee-Heavy Aerodrome LP for ST0x's Tokenized NVIDIA Asset Emerges on Base

An Aerodrome Slipstream USDC/wtNVDA pool adds a parallel ST0x tokenized-equity lane on Base. DeFi Terminal showed 86.26% APY, split into 57.65% base and 28.61% rewards, with about $31,800 TVL. DefiLlama showed 131.37% APY and about $28,642 TVL, highlighting a material refresh or aggregation gap.

Market Watch

NVDAc Emerges as a Quote Asset for a Meme Token Market on Base

A Uniswap V4 market on Base pairs BLUECHIP directly with NVDAc, Coinbase's tokenized NVIDIA stock. BLUECHIP's official site identifies the BLUECHIP/NVDAc pair on o1 Launchpad, while a nearby GeckoTerminal snapshot showed roughly $476,000 in liquidity and about $1.0 million in 24-hour volume. The structure shows a tokenized equity being used not merely as a stock-linked asset or USDC LP component, but as the quote asset for another speculative token.
