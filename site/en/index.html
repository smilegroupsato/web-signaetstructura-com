Market Watch

Beefy MSTRc strategy on Base shows a sharp APY and TVL data-provider divergence

Data-provider readings for the USDC-MSTRc Beefy strategy on Base have diverged sharply. The Moo strategy is shown at 29.07% APY on DefiLlama versus 285.46% on Portals, a roughly 9.8x gap. The Reward strategy is aligned near 149.36% APY, but TVL differs by about 8.2x: roughly $28.59k on DefiLlama versus $233.18k on Portals.

Market Watch

Aerodrome USDC/MSFTc shows 946.76% APY on the pool page while other surfaces show 144%–239%

On Base, DefiLlama's individual Aerodrome Slipstream USDC/MSFTc pool page displayed $822,690 in TVL and 946.76% Supply APY, split into 102.45% base APY and 844.31% AERO rewards. About 89.2% of the displayed yield therefore came from AERO incentives. At the same observation, DefiLlama's MSFTc aggregate surface displayed $876,204 in TVL and 238.56% APY (85.91% base + 152.65% rewards), while DeFi Terminal displayed roughly $950,000 in TVL and 143.72% APY for the same market.

Market Watch

Arcadia expands Base tokenized stocks into leveraged LP strategies

Arcadia Finance has publicly highlighted new tokenized-stock pools powered by Aerodrome and Base for AMZNc, MSFTc, TSLAc, MSTRc, SNDKc, SPCXc and MAG7. Arcadia can place supported LPs inside margin accounts, combining borrowed capital with concentrated liquidity, auto-compounding and automated rebalancing.

SPCXc AERO rewards surge on Aerodrome and propagate into Beefy vault yields

On Base, displayed yield for the Aerodrome Slipstream USDC/SPCXc pool rose sharply while TVL remained broadly stable, driven mainly by AERO rewards. The repricing also propagated into Beefy's Moo vault and Reward Cow built on the same liquidity venue.
