Market Watch

Morpho METAc-backed USDC rates normalize from roughly 17% to roughly 5%

Morpho's METAc-backed USDC market on Base now displays a 5.07% lender rate, 5.64% borrower rate and 90.21% utilization. The public observation on 2026-09-19 recorded 16.98% for lenders, 17.52% for borrowers and roughly 97% utilization, indicating that the double-digit rate spike associated with above-target utilization has faded and the market has returned close to its 90% target-utilization range. No new external incentive was identified.
