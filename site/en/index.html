Reading the Tomb Fork Boom in Data
8.7× in 22 Days, Then Rapid Deceleration

From late 2021 into early 2022, Tomb-style forks proliferated on Fantom. Contemporary snapshots place 2OMB TVL rising from about $9.2M to $80M in roughly 22 days. This study reconstructs the asymmetry between build-up and breakdown from available evidence.
