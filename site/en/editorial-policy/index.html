Standards

Editorial Policy

Publication quality, evidence, conflicts, legal and regulatory risk, and correction status are managed through explicit gates separate from drafting.

Publish Gate

We verify metadata, evidence, factual accuracy, research timing, media rendering and provenance. Saving a document in GitHub does not constitute approval to publish.

Compliance Gate

We review individualized buy/sell recommendations, profit guarantees, FOMO language, price inducement, conflicts, affiliate or sponsorship relationships, unverified information, and potential legal or regulatory issues.

Human approval

Initial publishing is not automatic. Even after both gates pass, the Publication Owner confirms target media, approved SHA and publishing settings before release.

Corrections & withdrawals

When significant doubt arises, temporary suspension takes priority over continued distribution. Corrections and withdrawals preserve the prior SHA, reason for change and publication record.

Conflicts

Where holdings, compensation, affiliate relationships or sponsorship may affect interpretation, appropriate disclosures are added before publication.
