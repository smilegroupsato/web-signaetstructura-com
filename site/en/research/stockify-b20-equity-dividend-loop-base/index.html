Stockify converts trading fees into Base B20 equities and distributes them directly to holders

Stockify converts STFY trading fees into Coinbase B20 tokenized equities and distributes them to eligible holders. Its ETH/STFY Uniswap v4 market charges a 3% hook fee, with 90% directed to equity acquisition. The active index was 25% each NVDAc, AAPLc, GOOGLc and METAc; the distribution desk showed 317 settled cycles and $69,736.08 distributed.

What changed

Stockify is newly observed in this Market Watch. Its official site lists a 13-asset Base B20 universe and an active dividend index weighted 25% each to NVDAc, AAPLc, GOOGLc and METAc. The distribution desk reports 317 settled cycles and $69,736.08 distributed to 328 eligible holders. The technical documentation lists deployed Base mainnet contracts, while the site footer still states 'Pre-launch. No STFY market is live.' GeckoTerminal, however, shows an STFY/ETH Uniswap v4 pool that has existed for roughly 13 days and displayed about $68,900 in 24-hour volume near the observation time, creating a visible status-update discrepancy across public sources.

Why it matters

Tokenized-stock composability is expanding beyond equity-price exposure, stock/USDC liquidity pools, collateral and carry vaults. Here, tokenized equities serve as direct distribution assets funded by the trading fees of a separate token. Stockify also describes an IndexFactory / IndexTreasury product that can route launch-derived fee streams into a fixed equity basket for holder distribution or into token burns. This provides an example of tokenized equities being integrated as protocol-revenue settlement assets rather than only as collateral or quote assets.

Limitations

Stockify is not a conventional yield vault and does not publish a comparable vault TVL or APY, so cumulative distributions are not annualized here. Holder-level realized returns vary with STFY trading volume, STFY price, eligible supply, index composition and B20 asset prices. The official documentation states that the code is unaudited, and the owner can change the venue allowlist, eligibility threshold and index policy. Holder snapshots may span multiple transactions and are therefore not always single-block atomic. B20 transfer policies and jurisdictional eligibility can affect whether distributions are receivable. The technical documentation and distribution desk indicate a live deployment while the site footer still contains a pre-launch message, and GeckoTerminal shows an active pool, so public status labels are inconsistent. GeckoTerminal also reports a negative liquidity figure for STFY/ETH; that value is excluded from this observation.
