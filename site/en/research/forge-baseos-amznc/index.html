Forge adds 10 Base stock tokens as pair assets as BASEOS/AMZNc goes live

Forge Launchpad says AMZNc, TSLAc, GOOGLc, SNDKc, MSTRc, NVDAc, SPCXc, METAc, MSFTc and AAPLc are now available as Base pair assets with dividend capabilities. A live BASEOS/AMZNc Uniswap V4 pool is also visible, with roughly $4.27K of liquidity and about $520 of 24-hour volume at observation time. The setup extends tokenized stocks beyond standalone holdings, collateral and conventional LP legs into quote assets for third-party tokens and holder-reward designs.

What changed

This patrol newly captured Forge's multi-pair support for all 10 Coinbase Tokenized Stocks currently highlighted on Base together with its stated dividend capability. A live BASEOS/AMZNc Uniswap V4 pool was also confirmed, showing about $4,266 of liquidity, roughly $520 of 24-hour volume, four 24-hour transactions and an age of about one day. The exact launch time cannot be established as occurring after the immediately previous patrol.

Why it matters

Tokenized-stock composability on Base is expanding beyond stock-token vaults, collateral and USDC/WETH liquidity pools. Forge's design allows another token to be issued and traded against a stock token while adding holder-reward or dividend functionality. The current BASEOS/AMZNc market remains very small and no APY or realized reward rate could be independently quantified, so the main observation is the emergence of the composition itself rather than market scale.

Limitations

Forge's public surfaces did not independently expose the reward formula, distribution frequency, funding source or eligibility rules behind the stated dividend capability, and no APY for BASEOS/AMZNc was verified. A cached GeckoTerminal snapshot from another update point showed about $4.89K of liquidity, $11.88K of 24-hour volume and 140 transactions, indicating substantial differences caused by rolling windows and update timing. The market is extremely small and remains exposed to launch-token price and liquidity risk, Uniswap V4 and Forge configuration or contract risk, Coinbase Tokenized Stocks issuer/custody/redemption constraints, and the mismatch between underlying equity market hours and a 24/7 DEX.
