Aerodrome NVDAc/USDC returns to a four-digit APY display as provider divergence reopens

On Base, DefiLlama's pool page for Aerodrome Slipstream USDC-NVDAc displayed a 1,693.43% supply APY, split into 82.81% base yield and 1,610.62% AERO rewards, with about $2.2 million in TVL. DeFi Terminal simultaneously displayed the same pool at 97.30% APY, split into 14.95% base and 82.35% rewards, with about $2.6 million in TVL. The headline APY gap has widened again to 1,596.13 percentage points, or roughly 17.4x.

2026-09-10T19:32:44+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

The public observation in this series at 2026-09-09 06:21 JST recorded DefiLlama at 202.92% APY, split into 43.00% base and 159.92% AERO rewards, with about $2.3 million in TVL after the earlier four-digit spike had normalized. The current DefiLlama reading is 1,693.43%, up 1,490.51 percentage points, while the AERO reward component alone increased from 159.92% to 1,610.62%, a gain of 1,450.70 points. TVL only slipped from about $2.3 million to about $2.2 million. At the same observation time, DeFi Terminal displayed 97.30% for the same pool, reopening a large provider gap.

Why it matters

Roughly 95% of the current four-digit DefiLlama headline APY is attributed to AERO rewards, and the reward annualization expanded sharply without a comparable collapse in TVL. DeFi Terminal, which labels its source as DefiLlama, shows the same pool at a rate roughly 17.4 times lower. The recurrence after the September 9 normalization reinforces that headline APY in this market is highly sensitive to AERO emission allocation, annualization windows and data-update timing rather than representing a stable tokenized-equity carry rate.

Limitations

APY is a variable annualized metric that can change sharply with reward allocation, observation windows, update timing and aggregation methods. DefiLlama and DeFi Terminal currently show materially different readings for the same pool, so the headline APY should not be treated as a single executable yield. Concentrated liquidity adds range-out and IL/LVR risk, while gauge and weekly emission allocations can change. NVDAc also carries issuer, custody, redemption, eligibility and jurisdictional constraints, plus a timing mismatch between 24/5 underlying equity trading and 24/7 Base markets.
