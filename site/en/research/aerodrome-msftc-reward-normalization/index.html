Aerodrome USDC/MSFTc APY contracts from four digits to around 180%

On Base, DefiLlama's individual Aerodrome Slipstream USDC/MSFTc pool view fell from 946.76% APY at the 2026-09-16 04:17 JST observation to 179.54%. TVL increased 8.8%, from $822,690 to $895,239, while the AERO reward component fell from 844.31% to 118.51%, indicating that the move was driven primarily by reward-rate normalization rather than capital outflow.

What changed

On the same DefiLlama individual pool surface, Supply APY fell 767.22 percentage points, or about 81.0%, from 946.76% to 179.54%. TVL rose by $72,549, or about 8.8%, from $822,690 to $895,239. Base APY declined from 102.45% to 61.04%, while AERO reward APY fell from 844.31% to 118.51%. The current DefiLlama RWA aggregate surface shows the pool at $881,231 TVL and 206.44% APY, split into 58.11% base and 148.33% rewards, bringing the two DefiLlama surfaces much closer together. The related Beefy Moo vault is shown at about $169.4K TVL and 91.90% APY by DefiLlama and about $170.6K TVL and 91.94% APY by DeFi Terminal.

Why it matters

The four-digit headline APY did not behave like a durable yield level and appears to have been highly sensitive to AERO allocation, short annualization windows, and data-update timing. The current yield source remains concentrated-liquidity swap fees plus AERO rewards, not stock carry generated by MSFTc itself. Beefy auto-compounds the same underlying LP economics, so changes in upstream rewards can propagate into downstream vault yields.

Limitations

Displayed APYs can differ across data providers, aggregation surfaces, update times, and short annualization windows. AERO rewards can change sharply with epoch and allocation changes, and a displayed base-plus-reward total may not map directly to a single realizable position. Concentrated-liquidity LPs carry range-out, IL/LVR, and liquidity risks. MSFTc also carries issuer, custody, redemption, eligibility, and basis risks arising from the mismatch between 24/5 equity markets and 24/7 onchain trading.
