LAPTOP/AAPLc APY rebounds on Aerodrome, led by AERO incentives

On Base, Aerodrome Slipstream's concentrated-liquidity LAPTOP/AAPLc pool saw TVL rise about 28.8% and headline APY increase about 59.8% between 13:30 and 19:45 JST on 2026-09-12. Most of the APY rebound came from a renewed increase in AERO incentives.

2026-09-12T19:45:01+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

DefiLlama observations moved from $28,478 to $36,687 in TVL and from 12,041.12% to 19,240.65% in total APY. Base APY rose from 2,615.96% to 3,135.92%, while AERO reward APY rose from 9,425.16% to 16,104.73%. AERO rewards now account for about 83.7% of the displayed APY.

Why it matters

This market uses tokenized equity AAPLc as a liquidity asset against a highly volatile token, and its headline yield is changing sharply over short intervals. The rebound does not reflect improved dividend or carry from AAPLc itself; it is mainly driven by annualized concentrated-LP trading fees and changes in AERO incentives.

Evidence

  • DefiLlama — Observed the LAPTOP-AAPLc pool at $36,687 TVL, 19,240.65% total APY, 3,135.92% base APY and 16,104.73% AERO reward APY. Versus the 13:30 JST observation, TVL increased 28.8%, total APY 59.8%, and reward APY 70.9%. 2026-09-12T19:45:01+09:00
  • CoinGecko — CoinGecko showed $654,624 in 24-hour volume for the LAPTOP/AAPLC market on Aerodrome Slipstream. CoinGecko volume and DefiLlama APY/TVL use different aggregation and update timings and should not be assumed to represent an identical snapshot. 2026-09-12T19:45:01+09:00

Limitations

The pool is small and metrics can move sharply over short intervals. Headline APY depends heavily on annualized short-window trading fees and AERO emissions and does not imply sustainable yield. Key limitations include LAPTOP price volatility, concentrated-liquidity IL/LVR and range-out risk, changes to AERO rewards, AAPLc issuer/custody/redemption/eligibility constraints, and the mismatch between equity-market hours and 24/7 crypto trading. Data providers also differ in aggregation scope and update timing.
