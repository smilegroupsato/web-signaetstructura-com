Aerodrome LAPTOP/AAPLc APY drops again as AERO rewards shrink about 70%

On Base, the Aerodrome Slipstream LAPTOP/AAPLc concentrated-liquidity pool saw TVL fall about 16.8% and displayed APY decline about 64.0% from the previous observation at 19:45 JST on 2026-09-12. The largest change came from AERO reward APY, which fell about 70.0%.

2026-09-12T21:27:48+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

DefiLlama's current observation shows $30,536 TVL, 6,935.27% total APY, 2,099.92% base APY and 4,835.36% AERO reward APY. Versus the 19:45 JST observation ($36,687 TVL, 19,240.65% total APY, 3,135.92% base APY and 16,104.73% reward APY), TVL is down 16.8%, total APY 64.0%, base APY 33.0% and reward APY 70.0%. AERO rewards now account for about 69.7% of displayed APY, down from about 83.7%.

Why it matters

In a market where tokenized Apple stock AAPLc is used as an LP asset against a highly volatile third-party token, headline APY continues to swing sharply within hours. The latest decline reflects short-window fee annualization and changing AERO incentives rather than a change in AAPLc dividends or stock carry, reinforcing the low persistence of the displayed yield.

Limitations

The pool is small and annualized APY can move sharply with changes in TVL, fees and AERO emissions. Displayed APY does not represent a guaranteed future realized return. Major limitations include LAPTOP price volatility, concentrated-liquidity IL/LVR and range-out risk, changes in AERO rewards, AAPLc issuer/custody/redemption/eligibility constraints, and the mismatch between equity-market hours and 24/7 crypto trading. DefiLlama and CoinGecko also differ in aggregation scope and update timing.
