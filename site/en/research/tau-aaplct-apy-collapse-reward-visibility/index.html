TAU AAPLCT displayed APY falls from 10.27% to 0.34% while TVL stays nearly flat

TAU/IPOR Fusion's Apple Carry Trade (AAPLCT) on Base now shows 0.34% APY on its DefiLlama pool page, down from 10.27% in the public observation on 2026-09-17, while TVL moved only from $38,186 to $38,006. The prior 10.27% reading included 10.00 percentage points of USDC rewards, so the current move should be treated as a change in reward visibility, eligibility or data refresh rather than a capital outflow. Meta Carry Trade (METACT) simultaneously shows 0.52% APY with $10,605 TVL.

2026-09-21T15:58:01+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

AAPLCT's displayed APY fell by 9.93 percentage points from 10.27% to 0.34%, a decline of about 96.7%, while TVL decreased only about 0.47% from $38,186 to $38,006. The 2026-09-17 observation showed the APY split as 0.27% base + 10.00% reward; the 2026-09-21 DefiLlama direct pool view no longer displays that 10% reward component. METACT currently shows 0.52% APY and $10,605 TVL.

Why it matters

TAU's tokenized-stock carry structure combines underlying carry from stock-backed strategies with external incentives, and rewards can account for most of the headline APY. This observation shows that route-specific eligibility, reward treatment or provider refresh timing can materially change the displayed yield even when vault capital is stable, making it important to separate base carry from external rewards.

Limitations

No official announcement from TAU Labs, IPOR or CURATED confirming the termination of the 10% USDC reward for AAPLCT or METACT was found in this observation. A public observation on 2026-09-16 described the extra 10% APR as CURATED-only, so the discrepancy could reflect reward expiry, route or eligibility differences, or a DefiLlama refresh/display difference. This brief therefore does not claim that the 10% incentive has ended across all routes. The carry structure also remains exposed to variable borrowing costs, liquidation and oracle risk, smart-contract risk, tokenized-stock issuer/custody/redemption/eligibility constraints, and the timing mismatch between 24/5 equity markets and 24/7 Base markets.
