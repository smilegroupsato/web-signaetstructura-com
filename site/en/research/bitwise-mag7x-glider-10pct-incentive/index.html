Bitwise Mag7X Tokenized-Stock Portfolio Carries a 10% APR USDC Incentive

The Bitwise-designed, Glider-executed Mag7X Automated Token Portfolio is live on Base, and Merkl shows a 10% APR USDC incentive with about $508,000 in eligible TVL. The structure differs from a single-stock vault or LP: tokenized equities remain in a self-custodied portfolio that is automatically rebalanced, with an external reward layered on top.

2026-09-04T01:45:24+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

Merkl shows a live 10% APR campaign for the Mag7X ATP. At observation time, TVL was $507.76K, daily rewards were 139.147 USDC, and total campaign rewards were 39.4K USDC. The campaign runs from August 24 to October 23, 2026, with a $300 minimum and rewards capped to balances up to $50,000. Bitwise public materials describe Mag7X as an equal-weight model targeting the Magnificent Seven plus SpaceX, with Glider rebalancing Coinbase tokenized stocks inside user wallets.

Why it matters

Tokenized-stock composition on Base is expanding beyond single-name holdings, LPs and collateralized carry into self-custodied model portfolios with automated rebalancing and time-limited USDC incentives. The displayed 10% is an externally funded Merkl incentive APR, not dividend yield or trading-fee income from the stocks themselves, so it should be separated from sustainable post-campaign return.

Limitations

Merkl's 10% figure is an annualized, time-limited incentive rate and does not imply a 10% return over the campaign period. The displayed TVL is Merkl's reward-eligible balance and may not equal total assets managed through Glider. Mag7X ultimately targets eight names, but actual tokenized-stock availability depends on Coinbase issuance and eligibility, so constituents can be added or changed. The 0.15% methodology fee plus trading and Glider fees can reduce net returns. Equity-market volatility, issuer/custody and redemption risk, jurisdiction eligibility, session authorization, rebalance execution, and the mismatch between 24/5 equity pricing and 24/7 onchain markets remain material limitations. Portals' Base Stocks page did not provide a directly comparable APY/TVL entry for this ATP at observation time, so the current metric snapshot relies primarily on Merkl.
