Harvest Finance Base stock wrappers jump from dust-scale TVL to roughly $5K each

Harvest Finance's FARM_METAc, FARM_AAPLc, FARM_NVDAc and FARM_GOOGLc wrappers on Base have grown from roughly $34-$369 of TVL in the public 2026-09-07 observation to about $4,651-$5,855 each. Portals shows most of each wrapper's current balance arriving within the latest 24-hour window.

2026-09-10T12:23:49+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

Versus the public 2026-09-07 observation, FARM_METAc rose from about $86 to $5,854.79 TVL (about 68.1x), FARM_AAPLc from about $151 to $5,586.82 (about 37.0x), FARM_NVDAc from about $369 to $5,658.11 (about 15.3x), and FARM_GOOGLc from about $34 to $4,650.67 (about 136.8x). Displayed APYs declined over the same comparison: 106.39% to 100.18%, 76.94% to 57.35%, 63.52% to 61.33%, and 48.91% to 43.34%, respectively. Portals shows latest-24h deposits of $5,379.63 for METAc, $5,420.27 for AAPLc, $4,899.86 for NVDAc, and $4,614.75 for GOOGLc.

Why it matters

These wrappers were previously little more than dust-scale positions, but now each has several thousand dollars of live balance. The underlying TAU/IPOR Fusion carry vaults have been described as targeting 10% from an external reward program distributed via Merkl, leaving a large gap versus the 43%-100% APYs displayed on the Harvest wrappers. That gap makes APY methodology, share-price movement and reward accounting important before treating the headline rates as persistent economic yield.

Limitations

Absolute TVL remains small at roughly $4.7K-$5.9K per Harvest wrapper, so a small number of deposits or withdrawals can move the metrics materially. The Portals APYs of 43%-100% could not be fully decomposed into share-price movement, reward recognition and measurement-window effects using a same-time independent data provider, so the displayed APYs should not be treated as confirmed sustainable realized yield.
