A GOOGLc-quoted meme market gains real liquidity on Base

RAWR trades on Base against GOOGLc, Coinbase's tokenized Alphabet stock, using the equity token as the quote asset. RAWR's official site makes the GOOGLc denomination central to the market design. Market aggregators showed roughly $140,000 of liquidity and $400,000-$600,000+ of 24-hour volume, extending tokenized stocks into crypto-native price discovery.

2026-09-03T02:45:01+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

This Market Watch had already observed a Base market using NVDAc as a quote asset. The new candidate is RAWR/GOOGLc, which uses a different Coinbase tokenized stock, GOOGLc, as the quote asset. RAWR's official site explicitly identifies the RAWR/GOOGLc market, and CoinGecko displayed the Uniswap V4 pair as RAWR's largest trading venue. Retrieved figures differed materially across providers: CoinGecko showed about $405,560 of 24-hour volume; DEX Screener showed about $141,000 of liquidity and $444,000 of 24-hour volume; and a GeckoTerminal related-pool listing showed about $146,070 of liquidity and $663,230 of 24-hour volume, indicating substantial timing or methodology differences.

Why it matters

Tokenized equities are beginning to function not only as stock exposure but as quote assets used to price other onchain tokens. Seeing this structure recur with GOOGLc, after an earlier NVDAc-quoted market, suggests the pattern is broader than a one-off experiment. It is an example of tokenized stocks entering crypto-native market structure directly. RAWR/GOOGLc is a meme-token market rather than a yield vault, however, so trading volume should not be interpreted as a depositor APY.

Limitations

RAWR/GOOGLc is a meme-token trading market rather than a yield vault, and the reported volume or swap fees do not by themselves establish an achievable LP APY. Provider estimates for 24-hour volume ranged from roughly $400,000 to $663,000, likely reflecting differences in update timing, pool identification or aggregation methodology. RAWR carries meme-token price, liquidity and smart-contract/Uniswap V4 hook risks. GOOGLc also carries issuer and custody risk, eligibility and mint/redemption constraints, and timing risk from a 24/5 reference-price infrastructure interacting with 24/7 DEX trading.
