New DiamondPepe/NVDAc market on Base extends tokenized stocks as quote assets

What changed

This Market Watch has already documented BLUECHIP/NVDAc and RAWR/GOOGLc as examples of tokenized stocks being used as quote assets for crypto-native tokens. A new DiamondPepe/NVDAc market has now appeared on Uniswap V4 on Base. GeckoTerminal refreshes during the observation window showed roughly $47.6K-$58.5K of liquidity, about $2.02M-$2.03M of 24-hour volume, and around 9,600 transactions. DEX Screener also identified the same Base/Uniswap v4 pool, showing about $47K of liquidity and a creation time about one day and five hours earlier, while its 24-hour panel showed only about $165K of volume and 1,157 transactions, creating a very large data-provider discrepancy.

Why it matters

Tokenized stocks are being used beyond vaults, USDC-denominated LPs, carry strategies, and collateral. They are increasingly appearing as pricing and liquidity legs in crypto-native speculative markets. The DiamondPepe/NVDAc pool is small, but it is another newly created market that directly uses NVDAc as the quote-side asset. This is not a yield vault and no public APY is available. If external LPs receive trading revenue, swap fees would be the source of return, but the public pages reviewed do not establish the pool fee, hook behavior, fee recipient, or external LP participation terms for this specific Uniswap V4 market.

Limitations

No official protocol documentation or clearly identified issuer for DiamondPepe was found, and additional token information is limited. This pool is not a yield vault and no public APY was verified. The Uniswap V4 pool fee, hook behavior, fee recipient, and external LP participation terms could not be established from the public pages reviewed. GeckoTerminal and DEX Screener show order-of-magnitude differences in volume and transaction aggregates, and liquidity also changed across refreshes within the same provider, so headline turnover should not be treated as a settled figure. Public information is insufficient to determine how much of the reported activity reflects organic trading, bot activity, or other factors. Liquidity is only around $50K, creating substantial price-impact and liquidity risk, alongside DiamondPepe price volatility and smart-contract/hook risk. NVDAc also carries issuer/custody, eligibility, mint/redemption, jurisdiction, and 24/5 equity-reference versus 24/7 DEX trading risks.
