Aerodrome Tokenized-Stock LPs Show Four-Digit APYs

Aerodrome Slipstream tokenized-stock/USDC pools on Base showed another sharp yield spike. DefiLlama displayed AAPLc/USDC at 1,450.92%, NVDAc/USDC at 1,374.37% and GOOGLc/USDC at 954.33%, with AERO rewards accounting for most of each figure.

2026-09-02T10:36:13+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

GOOGLc/USDC rose from the prior observation of 412.17% to 954.33%, while TVL increased from about $157,000 to $722,527. Base APY fell from 105.56% to 87.95%, but AERO rewards rose from 306.61% to 866.39%. At the same observation time, AAPLc/USDC showed 103.55% base plus 1,347.37% AERO rewards, while NVDAc/USDC showed 241.11% base plus 1,133.26% AERO rewards.

Why it matters

The move was not isolated to GOOGLc: several tokenized-stock LPs showed reward-driven yield spikes at the same time. Headline APY therefore needs to be decomposed into base fees and AERO incentives and read alongside TVL and data-update differences.

Limitations

Displayed APYs can change rapidly and do not guarantee future yield. These figures were heavily dependent on AERO rewards and could fall with incentive reallocation. DefiLlama's exact-pool and RWA pages differed materially on GOOGLc APY and TVL, while Dexonomics also showed different liquidity figures. Concentrated-liquidity range/IL risk and tokenized-stock issuer/custody, eligibility and off-hours pricing risks remain.
