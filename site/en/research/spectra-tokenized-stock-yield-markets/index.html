Spectra brings PT/YT fixed-term rate markets to Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base

Spectra is offering fixed-maturity markets on Base for AAPLc, NVDAc, METAc and GOOGLc that split yield generated by TAU Stock Carry Vaults into Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT). On Portals' dedicated market pages, the AAPLc, NVDAc and METAc markets maturing on 2026-09-30 show roughly $4.77K-$5.75K of TVL and displayed APYs of 12.48%-15.86%.

2026-09-17T11:26:26+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

This observation newly captures a tokenized-stock fixed-maturity PT/YT structure that had not previously been reported in this watch. Portals' dedicated pages show AAPLc at $5,746.33 TVL and 15.86% APY, NVDAc at $4,770.07 and 14.48%, and METAc at $5,703.12 and 12.48%, with $0 of one-day volume on each page. The Portals Base Stocks aggregate page simultaneously shows PT-fAAPLc at 15.79%, PT-fNVDAc at 16.62%, and PT-fMETAc at 17.26%, creating a visible APY discrepancy across data surfaces.

Why it matters

The structure turns tokenized-stock carry into separable principal value and floating yield, enabling fixed-maturity stock-denominated rate exposure, YT-based yield exposure and PT/YT liquidity provision. Public materials also describe PT-stock collateral and looping as possible downstream constructions, but this observation does not verify those compositions as live.

Limitations

Each market is small, with only about $4.8K-$5.7K of TVL; dedicated pages show $0 of one-day volume, and maturity is 2026-09-30. APYs differ between Portals' aggregate and dedicated market pages, potentially reflecting update timing or calculation differences. No Spectra-specific incremental incentive was independently verified in this observation. Risks include PT/YT pricing and maturity mechanics, TAU/Fusion/Morpho borrow-rate, oracle, liquidation and smart-contract risk, tokenized-stock issuer/custody/redemption/eligibility risk, and the timing mismatch between 24/5 equity markets and 24/7 Base markets. PT-stock collateral and looping are described publicly as possible constructions but were not verified as live in this observation.
