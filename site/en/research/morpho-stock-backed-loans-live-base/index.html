Morpho activates variable- and fixed-rate loans against Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base

Morpho says stock-backed loans are now live on Base using Coinbase Tokenized Stocks as collateral. Users can lend and borrow at variable and fixed rates, while individual curators set market parameters and compliance controls. DefiLlama's AAPLc dashboard currently attributes $45,097 of DeFi Active TVL to Morpho Blue.

2026-09-18T22:09:08+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

The previous observation established that TAU Stock Carry Vaults were actively borrowing USDC through SteakhouseFi-curated Morpho markets. Morpho has now announced the broader protocol-level availability of stock-backed credit markets for Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base, explicitly supporting both variable and fixed rates. This moves tokenized equities from being an input to a carry vault into a directly surfaced collateral class for onchain credit. At the observation time, DefiLlama showed AAPLc at $2.05M onchain AUM and $642,215 of DeFi Active TVL, including $45,097 attributed to Morpho Blue. Portals, however, still labeled its Base Stocks Stock Lending section as “Coming soon,” indicating an aggregation/update lag across public data surfaces.

Why it matters

The Base tokenized-stock yield market is expanding beyond LPs, reward vaults and managed carry strategies into direct stock-backed lending. Lender yield is sourced from interest paid by borrowers, and the emergence of variable and fixed-rate credit curves can support further compositions such as stock-backed liquidity, leverage, carry and maturity-based structured products. No new external incentive was independently confirmed in this observation.

Limitations

Morpho's announcement confirms protocol-level availability, but the individual stock-backed market IDs, supported collateral list, supply and borrow APYs, fixed-rate maturities, utilization and per-market TVL were not consistently identifiable from public surfaces at this observation. The $45,097 shown for Morpho Blue on DefiLlama's AAPLc dashboard is an AAPLc protocol attribution and should not be read as TVL for all markets covered by the launch. Portals still labels Stock Lending as coming soon, showing that public aggregators are updating at different times. Major limitations and risks include curator-specific collateral parameters, oracle and liquidation mechanics, variable rates, smart-contract risk, the timing mismatch between 24/5 equity reference markets and 24/7 Base markets, and Coinbase Tokenized Stock issuer, custody, redemption, eligibility and jurisdictional constraints.
