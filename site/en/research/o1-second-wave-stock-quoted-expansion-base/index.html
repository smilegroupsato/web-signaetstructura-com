o1 Launchpad Extends Stock-Quoted Markets Across Base's Six Second-Wave Tokenized Stocks

o1 Launchpad markets on Base now use all six second-wave Coinbase Tokenized Stocks — AMZNc, MSFTc, MSTRc, SNDKc, SPCXc and TSLAc — as quote assets for B20 tokens. The six non-synchronous pool snapshots retrieved for this observation total roughly $175,000 in liquidity and $641,000 in 24-hour volume. The composition extends tokenized-stock use beyond USDC liquidity and collateral into pricing and settlement assets for other onchain tokens.

2026-09-06T14:10:11+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

This watch had previously recorded individual stock-quoted markets using first-wave assets such as NVDAc and GOOGLc. The newly observed expansion covers all six tokenized stocks added in the second wave. Representative snapshots include STC/TSLAc at roughly $55,500 liquidity and $193,700 24-hour volume, BLUE/MSFTc at about $22,200 and $185,300, and SDCARD/SNDKc at about $40,100 and $114,900. The pools were roughly 14 hours to one day old at observation time.

Why it matters

The rollout broadens the use of tokenized stocks as DeFi quote assets, creating multiple equity-denominated speculative markets and associated fee flows. This is not a conventional deposit vault, and no APY available to ordinary LP depositors was identified on the reviewed public surfaces. No external incentive was identified either. Swap fees are the observable economic source, but the pool-specific fee settings, beneficiaries and outside-LP access for these Base stock-paired routes cannot be established from currently updated public documentation.

Limitations

The six liquidity and volume readings are not synchronized to one block or identical timestamp, so their combined totals are approximate. These are launch markets rather than deposit-style yield vaults, and no general-user APY was identified. o1's public documentation does not yet reflect the observed Base stock-paired route, so pool-specific swap fees, fee recipients, outside-LP access and the exact scope of permanent-lock mechanics cannot be fully established from the reviewed public information. The B20 launch tokens are very small, high-volatility markets, while the tokenized-stock side also carries issuer/custody, eligibility, mint/redemption and off-hours price-dislocation constraints relative to underlying equity-market hours.
