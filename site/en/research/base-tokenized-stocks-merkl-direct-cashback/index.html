Base Tokenized-Stock Incentives Expand from LP Rewards to Direct Cashback

Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base are now being incentivized through direct USDC cashback for purchases held through an epoch, without requiring an LP or vault. Merkl listed up to 5% cashback via 1inch and up to 2% through Reserve and Definitive across AAPLc, GOOGLc, NVDAc and METAc opportunities.

2026-09-02T13:39:21+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

Earlier observations focused on LP and vault yields around Aerodrome and Beefy. This observation identified a separate incentive layer that rewards direct tokenized-stock purchases and holding. 1inch states that its campaign runs for eight weeks from 2026-08-26 in two-week epochs, paying 5% of qualifying purchase value in USDC, capped at $25 per wallet across the campaign. Merkl's current opportunity list showed 1inch cStocks at up to 5% cashback and Reserve and Definitive cStocks at up to 2%, with roughly five days remaining on the displayed opportunities.

Why it matters

Tokenized-stock returns are expanding beyond concentrated-LP fees and AERO emissions into customer-acquisition subsidies paid to holders. This avoids LP-specific impermanent loss but is not organic yield generated by the stock or DeFi activity; it is a temporary incentive that can influence trading volume, holding demand and headline return comparisons.

Limitations

The incentive program began on 2026-08-26; this observation reflects a newly captured current incentive layer, not a newly launched campaign after the prior patrol. Cashback rates, eligible apps, wallet caps, epoch conditions and remaining duration vary by campaign and can change. Merkl's aggregate Max APR is not equivalent to a simple cashback rate. Tokenized-stock price, issuer/custody, jurisdictional eligibility, mint/redemption, spread and slippage risks remain.
