Bitwise Mag7X crosses $1M as Merkl-eligible TVL rises about 94% from September 4

Bitwise Mag7X Automated Token Portfolio on Base has moved above the $1 million scale. Glider reported $1,020,271 across 1,585 users as of September 18, while the current Merkl opportunity shows a 10% APR, $983.64K of eligible TVL and 269.558 USDC of daily rewards. The September 4 public observation recorded $507.76K of Merkl-eligible TVL and 139.147 USDC per day, implying roughly 93.7% growth in both eligible TVL and daily rewards while the incentive rate itself remains unchanged at 10%.

What changed

At the September 4 observation, Merkl showed $507.76K of reward-eligible TVL, 139.147 USDC of daily rewards and a 10% Merkl APR. The current Merkl view shows $983.64K of eligible TVL, 269.558 USDC per day and the same 10% APR. Eligible TVL increased by $475.88K, or about 93.7%, while daily rewards rose by 130.411 USDC, also about 93.7%. Separately, Glider said on September 18 that Mag7X had reached $1,020,271 from 1,585 users. Glider's total is $36,631, or about 3.7%, above Merkl's eligible TVL, indicating a difference in measurement scope and/or update timing.

Why it matters

Mag7X is not a single-stock vault or LP. Users directly hold tokenized Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Nvidia, Meta, Tesla and SpaceX positions in their own accounts, while Glider rebalances them to Bitwise's model. The displayed 10% APR is funded by an external USDC incentive rather than organic stock yield; the underlying portfolio return remains driven by the market prices of the constituent tokenized stocks. Reward-eligible balances nearly doubling without a higher incentive rate shows a meaningful expansion in usage of this direct-to-wallet tokenized-stock composition.

Limitations

Merkl's $983.64K figure is incentive-eligible TVL, while Glider's $1,020,271 figure is a Glider portfolio total; the definitions and update times are not necessarily identical, so they should not be treated as the same TVL measure. The 10% figure is an annualized external USDC incentive that ends on October 23, 2026 and does not imply a 10% absolute return over the remaining campaign period or any post-campaign yield. The underlying portfolio is exposed to the price volatility of eight equities, as well as rebalance execution, slippage and turnover, the 0.15% methodology fee and 0.30% automated trading-volume fee, smart-account and session-authorization risk, tokenized-stock issuer/custody/redemption/eligibility constraints, and the mismatch between 24/5 equity markets and 24/7 Base markets. Tokenized stocks are restricted to eligible non-US persons in supported jurisdictions.
