Market Watch · Base Tokenized Stock Yield Watch

Meta-backed USDC rates on Morpho rise toward 17% while three peer stock markets sit near 5%

Market-level pricing has become visible for Morpho's USDC markets backed by Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base. Apple, Alphabet and Nvidia were reported near 90% utilization with roughly 5.06% lender APY and 5.62% borrower APY, while Meta reached about 97% utilization with 16.98% lender APY and 17.52% borrower APY. No new external incentive was identified; lender yield is funded by USDC interest paid by borrowers.

2026-09-19T00:16:50+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

The previous observation confirmed that Morpho's stock-backed lending was live on Base but did not consistently surface market-level terms. A new public report citing a Morpho API snapshot now details five variable-rate markets: AAPLc, GOOGLc and NVDAc were around 90% utilization at 5.06% lender APY and 5.62% borrower APY, while METAc was around 97% utilization at 16.98% lender APY and 17.52% borrower APY. Across the five markets, tokenized-stock collateral totaled $104,401, USDC supplied totaled $60,265 and borrowing totaled $54,652. The fixed-rate side has 19 markets for each of five collateral tokens, 95 in total, but all were reported with zero outstanding units.

Why it matters

Base now has observable stock-specific credit curves in which borrowing demand directly determines USDC lender yield. METAc has moved above the target utilization zone, pushing its variable-rate curve into a steeper section and producing a double-digit yield funded by borrower interest rather than token rewards. The markets remain small, however, and high utilization can materially constrain immediate lender withdrawal liquidity.

Limitations

The market-level rates in the public report are point-in-time Morpho API observations and can move quickly because the markets use variable interest rates. The directly observed NVDAc page already showed 5.12% lender APY and 5.69% borrower APY, a small timing difference from the reported snapshot. METAc's roughly 17% lender rate is associated with about 97% utilization and can fall sharply if borrowers repay or new liquidity enters. Total supplied liquidity across the five markets was only about $60K, so withdrawal liquidity may be constrained in highly utilized markets. Key risks and limitations include variable rates, liquidity, oracle behavior, LLTV and liquidation mechanics, smart-contract risk, the 24/5 equity-reference versus 24/7 Base timing mismatch, and Coinbase Tokenized Stock issuer, custody, redemption, eligibility and jurisdiction constraints. No new external incentive was identified.
