Aerodrome NVDAc/USDC APY reaccelerates as provider divergence widens again

On Base, DefiLlama's individual Aerodrome Slipstream USDC/NVDAc pool view rose from the prior 95.26% observation to 216.97% APY. TVL fell from roughly $2.54 million to $2.28 million, while both the base-fee and AERO-reward components increased. At the same observation time, public surfaces for the same pool ranged from roughly 83% to 351% headline APY.

2026-09-14T20:24:35+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

Compared with the 2026-09-14 16:04 JST observation of roughly $2.54 million TVL and 95.26% total APY (14.72% base + 80.54% AERO reward), DefiLlama's individual pool view now showed about $2.28 million TVL and 216.97% total APY (51.07% base + 165.90% reward). TVL decreased about 10.2%, while total APY rose 121.71 percentage points; base APY increased 36.35 points and reward APY increased 85.36 points. During the same observation window, DefiLlama's NVDAc aggregate surface showed about $2.26 million and 351.07% APY (49.87% base + 301.20% reward), while DeFi Terminal's direct page showed about $2.70 million and 83.29% APY (36.69% base + 46.60% reward).

Why it matters

The yield sources remain concentrated-liquidity swap fees plus AERO incentives, and this reacceleration includes a rise in the base-fee component rather than rewards alone. However, headline APY for the same pool diverged from roughly 83% to 351% across public surfaces, indicating that short-window fee annualization, AERO allocation, update timing and aggregation methodology can materially affect displayed yield. A single headline APY remains difficult to interpret as a stable realizable rate.

Limitations

APY can change rapidly with short-window swap volume, concentrated-liquidity ranges and AERO gauge/emission allocation, and does not guarantee future realized yield. DefiLlama's individual pool view, its RWA aggregate view and DeFi Terminal may differ in update timing, annualization and reward-accounting methodology, producing large discrepancies for the same pool. Concentrated liquidity carries impermanent-loss, LVR and range-out risks. NVDAc also carries issuer, custody, redemption and eligibility constraints, plus potential basis and oracle mismatches when the underlying equity market is closed while Base trades continuously.
