Why it matters

The upstream Aerodrome pool remains broadly aligned across data providers, while the same downstream Beefy strategy shows large differences in APY and Reward Pool TVL. That pattern suggests the headline discrepancy may reflect Beefy-layer harvest and compounding recognition, reward accounting, annualization windows, vault versus reward-pool scope, or data-refresh timing rather than a simple change in Aerodrome emissions alone. Yield comes from concentrated-liquidity trading fees, Aerodrome AERO emissions, external incentives such as Merkl campaigns, and Beefy Boost and compounding layers rather than from the tokenized stock's dividend carry itself.

Limitations

Portals, DefiLlama RWA, DefiLlama's direct pool view, and DeFi Terminal may use different refresh times, annualization windows, reward-recognition methods, and vault or reward-pool scopes. This observation does not independently verify the SPCXc current-epoch AERO, Merkl, or Boost budgets, remaining campaign duration, or recent harvest realization, so none of the headline APYs should be treated as persistent realized yield. Risks include concentrated-liquidity range-out and impermanent loss/LVR, Beefy/Aerodrome smart-contract and automation risk, SPCXc issuer/custody/redemption/eligibility risk, and basis effects between 24/5 equity markets and 24/7 Base trading.
