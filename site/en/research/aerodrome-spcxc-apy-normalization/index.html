Aerodrome USDC-SPCXc four-digit APY print falls to 237%

Aerodrome Slipstream's USDC-SPCXc pool on Base showed 237.10% displayed APY and about $1.07 million of TVL at the 2026-09-24 19:13 JST observation. The same pool had shown 1,017.04% and about $1.03 million of TVL at the 11:16 JST observation, so displayed APY fell by 779.94 percentage points, or about 76.7%, while TVL remained broadly stable.

2026-09-24T19:13:43+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

On DefiLlama's direct page for the same pool, displayed APY fell from 1,017.04% to 237.10%. The current breakdown is 23.44% base APY plus 213.66% AERO reward APY, compared with 41.96% base plus 975.09% AERO reward at the 11:16 JST observation; the displayed AERO reward component alone fell by 761.43 percentage points. TVL, meanwhile, increased by roughly 3.9% from about $1.03 million to $1.07 million. The corresponding Beefy Moo USDC-SPCXc wrapper shows 27.59% APY and $233,180 TVL, while the Revert-routed Merkl campaign across ten tokenized-stock pools shows 87.98% APR, $802,990 TVL and about 1,930 USDC of daily rewards.

Why it matters

Most of the headline APY decline came from the displayed AERO reward component rather than a capital exit, underscoring how strongly tokenized-stock LP headline yields on Aerodrome depend on gauge allocation, AERO pricing, annualization windows and update timing. Beefy's managed wrapper and Revert/Merkl's USDC campaign are separate layers with different scopes, so their displayed yields should not be added mechanically.

Limitations

Displayed APY is a provider metric and does not represent realized returns for a specific LP position. The 11:16 JST and 19:13 JST readings are observations of the same dynamic DefiLlama pool URL at different times, and TVL is rounded in the interface. Public data does not isolate how much of the rapid decline came from weekly gauge reallocation, AERO price, short-window annualization or update timing. Beefy is a managed wrapper and Merkl is a campaign-wide metric spanning ten pools, so neither has the same scope as the Aerodrome direct pool. Material limitations and risks include concentrated-liquidity range-out, IL/LVR, changes in AERO emissions, smart-contract and automation risk, tokenized-stock issuer/custody/redemption/eligibility risk, and the mismatch between 24/5 underlying-equity markets and 24/7 Base trading.
