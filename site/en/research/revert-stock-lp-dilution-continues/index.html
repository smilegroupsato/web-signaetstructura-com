Revert-routed Aerodrome tokenized-stock LP APR falls to 138.93% as TVL expands

In the Revert-routed Aerodrome tokenized-stock LP incentive campaign on Base, Merkl's opportunity list shows TVL rising from $410.6K at the 2026-09-23 10:03 JST observation to $508.5K at 14:19 JST, a 23.8% increase, while displayed APR fell from 172.05% to 138.93%, down 33.12 percentage points. Daily USDC rewards remained approximately $1.93K, indicating continued reward-density compression as participating liquidity grows.

2026-09-23T14:19:45+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

Between 2026-09-23 10:03 JST and 14:19 JST, TVL on Merkl's opportunity list increased by $97.9K from $410.6K to $508.5K (+23.8%), while displayed APR declined from 172.05% to 138.93% (-33.12 percentage points). Daily rewards remained about $1.93K. At the same observation time, Merkl's direct opportunity and campaign-detail surfaces still displayed the older 172.05% APR and $410.6K TVL, creating a same-provider update-lag discrepancy. The campaign detail showed 87 participants, 98% budget remaining and a total reward amount of 25.063K USDC.

Why it matters

Participating liquidity is increasing quickly while the daily USDC reward budget remains broadly fixed, compressing headline APR over a short period. Rewards are paid hourly based on fees actually earned by concentrated-liquidity positions opened through Revert, and only in-range liquidity earns, so the displayed APR should not be treated as a realized return for any individual position.

  • Revert Finance public announcement mirror — Revert stated that rewards for the eligible tokenized-stock/USDC LPs are paid hourly based on fees actually earned by positions opened through Revert, with in-range liquidity earning and out-of-range liquidity not earning. 2026-09-23T14:19:45+09:00

Limitations

Merkl's list and its direct opportunity/campaign-detail surfaces showed different APR and TVL values at the same observation time, indicating possible differences in refresh timing or aggregation scope. Headline APR is not the realized return of an individual LP position and can change with participating TVL, trading fees, range selection and time in range. Concentrated liquidity also carries range-out and impermanent-loss/LVR risk, while tokenized equities add the 24/5 underlying-price versus 24/7 Base-trading timing mismatch, Aerodrome/Revert smart-contract and automation risk, and tokenized-stock issuer, custody, redemption and eligibility constraints.
