Zyfai reports more than $225K of tokenized-stock deposits in one week

Zyfai says more than $225K of Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base were deposited through its platform in one week. Its displayed yields are up to 38.02% APY for NVDAc and 20.47% for AAPLc, GOOGLc and METAc, including incentives. The same public post says the additional USDC incentive for tokenized-stock depositors has about one week remaining.

2026-09-21T00:41:08+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

In the previous public observation on 2026-09-11, Zyfai had been identified as an automated allocation layer advertising up to 35% APY, but tokenized-stock-specific TVL or flow and the duration of the additional USDC reward could not be independently verified. Zyfai has now disclosed a concrete adoption metric of more than $225K deposited in tokenized stocks over one week, stock-specific headline yields of 38.02% for NVDAc and 20.47% for AAPLc, GOOGLc and METAc, and says the USDC incentive has roughly one week remaining. The $225K figure is a one-week deposit amount and should not be treated as current TVL.

Why it matters

The update indicates that capital is beginning to use a higher-level allocation layer that routes Base tokenized stocks into underlying yield venues rather than only depositing directly into individual vaults. The headline yield is not a durable equity yield: it combines returns and incentives at the underlying venues with Zyfai USDC incentives and rZFI rewards. Portals' Week 3 snapshot dated 2026-09-19 showed the underlying NVDA SuperVault at 17.18% APY and roughly $405K TVL, while Zyfai's NVDAc headline is 38.02%, a gap of about 20.84 percentage points. The observations are not directly comparable because timestamps and product scope differ and Zyfai includes additional incentive layers.

Limitations

The more-than-$225K figure is a one-week deposit amount reported by Zyfai and was not independently verified as current stock-specific TVL. The total USDC incentive budget, wallet cap, exact end timestamp, and realized value and vesting terms of rZFI could not be independently verified from the public surfaces retrieved in this observation. Zyfai's 38.02% headline and Portals' 17.18% snapshot differ in timestamp, product scope and reward composition, so they are not like-for-like APY readings. Displayed yield may compress sharply when incentives expire. Additional risks and limitations include the Zyfai smart-account, agent and routing layer, underlying Superform/IPOR and other protocol contracts, tokenized-stock issuer, custody, redemption and eligibility constraints, and the timing mismatch between 24/5 equity markets and 24/7 Base markets.
