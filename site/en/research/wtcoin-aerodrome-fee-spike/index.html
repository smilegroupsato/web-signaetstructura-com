Aerodrome wtCOIN/USDC displayed APY jumps above 2,194%

The ST0x wtCOIN/USDC Aerodrome Slipstream pool on Base is showing 2,194.44% APY on DeFiLlama, comprising 2,064.98% base APY from trading fees and 129.46% in AERO rewards, with about $43.3K of TVL. The most recent comparable RenditeMonitor snapshot available to this observation showed 583.22% APY and about $49K TVL, indicating a sharp rise in the displayed annualized rate.

What changed

DeFiLlama's current 2,194.44% reading is 1,611.22 percentage points higher, or about 3.76x, than the 583.22% reading on the RenditeMonitor snapshot labeled 06 Sep 2026 00:05. TVL is about $43.3K on DeFiLlama versus roughly $49K on RenditeMonitor, but these are different providers and snapshot times, so the difference should not be treated as confirmed net outflow.

Why it matters

Most of the headline increase is fee-derived base APY rather than an external subsidy. Dexonomics' page header shows roughly $44.0K of liquidity, about $813.0K of 24-hour volume and a 0.3% fee tier for the main Aerodrome Slipstream pool, a turnover-to-liquidity profile consistent with an unusually high short-window annualized fee rate. The same Dexonomics page, however, shows about $92.2K of liquidity and $1.96M of 24-hour volume in its markets table, highlighting material aggregation differences even within one provider surface.

Limitations

Displayed APY includes annualization of short-window trading activity and should not be read as a persistent realized yield. DeFiLlama, Dexonomics and RenditeMonitor use different snapshot times and aggregation methods, and Dexonomics itself shows materially different liquidity and volume figures between its header and markets table. Concentrated-liquidity risks include range-out, impermanent loss, LVR/adverse selection and changing AERO emissions. wtCOIN also carries issuer-credit, wrapper-exchange-rate, KYC-gated mint/redemption, access-restriction and 24/5 underlying-market versus 24/7 DEX timing risks.
