Beefy MSTRc APY gap across providers narrows from ~9.8x to ~1.4x

On Base, DefiLlama now shows the Beefy Moo USDC-MSTRc strategy at $194,096 TVL and 195.00% APY, while Portals shows about $205,380 TVL and 276.93% APY. The APY gap has narrowed to about 1.4x from roughly 9.8x (29.07% vs 285.46%) observed at 2026-09-16 08:32 JST.

2026-09-16T13:38:15+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

DefiLlama's Moo APY rose from 29.07% to 195.00%, up 165.93 percentage points (~6.7x), while TVL fell about 2.7% from $199,424 to $194,096. Portals moved only modestly: APY fell from 285.46% to 276.93% (-8.53pp) and TVL rose from $204,586 to about $205,380 (+0.4%). Upstream Aerodrome USDC-MSTRc shows $845,666 TVL and 4,166.44% APY (114.69% base + 4,051.75% AERO reward), with about 97.2% of headline APY coming from the AERO incentive.

Why it matters

The normalization is more consistent with provider update, annualization, or harvest timing catching up than with a sudden change in TVL or the Portals-side strategy economics. Short-window headline APYs for the same strategy can therefore remain highly source- and timing-sensitive.

Limitations

DefiLlama and Portals may differ in update timing, harvest recognition, annualization windows, vault/strategy scope, and valuation methodology. The MSTRc reward-layer TVL still differs materially in this observation, at about $27.8k on DefiLlama versus about $234.1k on Portals, and the two surfaces may not represent an identical scope. Aerodrome headline APY remains highly dependent on AERO incentives and can change sharply with epoch emissions. Other risks include concentrated-liquidity IL/LVR and range-out risk, Beefy/Aerodrome smart-contract and automation risk, MSTRc issuer/custody/redemption/eligibility risk, and basis risk between 24/5 equity markets and 24/7 Base markets.
