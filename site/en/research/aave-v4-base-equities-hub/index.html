Aave V4 on Base Defines an Equities Hub for Borrowing USDC Against Tokenized Stocks

Aave Labs updated its Base V4 ARFC on 2026-09-21 with an initial design and risk parameters for a dedicated Equities Hub. Coinbase B20 tokenized equities would be collateral-only assets, while USDC would be the sole borrowable asset. The initial set is AAPLc, AMZNc, GOOGLc, METAc, MSFTc, NVDAc and TSLAc, with a 32M USDC Add Cap and 21M USDC Draw Cap. The ARFC has been raised to Snapshot but the market is not yet live on Base.

What changed

Base tokenized-stock yield activity has so far centered on vaults, carry trades, LP positions and PT/YT markets. The updated Aave V4 proposal introduces a concrete lending composition in which tokenized stocks directly collateralize USDC debt inside a dedicated Equities Hub. The proposed Collateral Factors are 78% for AAPLc, 73% for AMZNc, 76% for GOOGLc, 65% for METAc, 79% for MSFTc, 70% for NVDAc and 65% for TSLAc. The proposed USDC rate curve has a 0% base rate, 90% optimal usage, 4% rate growth below optimal, 20% above optimal and a 10% liquidity fee.

Why it matters

This would make tokenized stocks usable not only as spot exposure, LP inventory or carry-vault inputs, but as collateral for drawing USDC from a major lending protocol on Base. If deployed, it could support tokenized-equity-collateral lending for USDC suppliers, stock-backed borrowing, and downstream integrations with vaults and aggregators. Because the market is still at the governance stage, there is no live TVL, realized supply APY, borrowing demand or launch incentive to observe yet.

Limitations

This is a governance-stage market design, not a live Aave V4 Equities Hub on Base, so there is no observable TVL, realized APY, borrow volume or incentive performance yet. The structure and parameters may change through Snapshot and AIP governance. The initial design relies on Chainlink 24/5 tokenized-equity feeds that hold the last value while the underlying equity market is closed, creating reopening-gap liquidation risk. Additional risks include issuer, custody, redemption and eligibility constraints for the tokenized stocks, secondary-market liquidity, oracle design, liquidations and smart-contract risk.
