Aerodrome USDC/NVDAc Four-Digit APY Display Contracts Back to the 80% Range

On Base, Aerodrome Slipstream USDC/NVDAc had displayed 1,396.25% APY on the DefiLlama individual-pool surface at 2026-09-15 04:16 JST. At 08:23 JST, a DeFi Terminal surface sourced from DefiLlama displayed 83.29% APY, with about $2.7M TVL, 36.69% base APY, and 46.60% reward APY. The observation strongly suggests that the four-digit print was not a persistent yield level.

2026-09-15T08:23:05+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

The prior observation on the DefiLlama individual-pool surface showed about $2.22M TVL, 1,396.25% total APY, 114.95% base APY, and 1,281.30% AERO reward APY. The current DeFi Terminal surface shows about $2.7M TVL, 83.29% total APY, 36.69% base APY, and 46.60% reward APY. Because the two observations come from different presentation surfaces, this is not a strict same-series comparison, but the currently accessible DefiLlama-sourced aggregate surface no longer shows the four-digit APY and the extreme reward-driven annualization has contracted.

Why it matters

The pool still has multi-million-dollar liquidity, while the four-digit headline APY is no longer visible on the current aggregate surface. This reinforces that very high tokenized-stock LP APYs can be driven by AERO reward allocation, short-window fee annualization, update timing, and aggregation methodology rather than stock-derived carry. Even at the current reading, rewards account for about 56% of the displayed total APY, so incentive dependence remains material.

Limitations

The current reading comes from a DeFi Terminal aggregate surface sourced from DefiLlama, while the 04:16 JST reading came from the DefiLlama individual-pool surface. The current DefiLlama individual-pool surface could not be retrieved under the same conditions during this observation, so this is not a strict same-interface time series. APY can change rapidly with short-window fee annualization, AERO emission allocation, update timing, and aggregation methodology. Concentrated LP positions face IL/LVR and range-out risk. NVDAc also carries issuer, custody, redemption, eligibility, and timing/basis risks between the 24/5 underlying equity market and 24/7 Base trading.
