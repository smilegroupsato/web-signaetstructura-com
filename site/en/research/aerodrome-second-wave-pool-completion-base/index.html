Aerodrome USDC liquidity now spans all six second-wave Base tokenized stocks

Aerodrome Slipstream 3 USDC pools are now observable for MSFTc, SNDKc and TSLAc, extending the second-wave Coinbase Tokenized Stock pool set beyond the previously confirmed AMZNc, MSTRc and SPCXc. The three newly observed pools show about $454,794 of combined displayed liquidity and about $1.572 million of combined 24-hour volume. No durable APY or independently confirmed AERO, Merkl or Beefy incentive has been established for the three newly observed pools.

What changed

At the public observation on 2026-09-05 03:43 JST, active Aerodrome USDC pools with trading were confirmed for three of the six second-wave names: AMZNc, MSTRc and SPCXc. The current observation adds MSFTc/USDC at $129,249.02 liquidity, $926,740 of 24-hour volume and a 0.018% fee; SNDKc/USDC at $182,843.45 liquidity, $319,100 of 24-hour volume and a 0.025% fee; and TSLAc/USDC at $142,701.29 liquidity, $325,860 of 24-hour volume and a 0.005% fee. The existing SPCXc/USDC pool now shows $170,351.44 of liquidity and about $1.9 million of 24-hour volume, compared with $44,954.13 and $225,747.04 respectively in the prior observation.

Why it matters

Base tokenized-stock liquidity has progressed from the original four names and a partial second wave to Aerodrome USDC markets covering all six newly added names: AMZNc, MSFTc, MSTRc, SNDKc, SPCXc and TSLAc. This broadens the substrate available for LP strategies and any future gauge, emissions or vault composition. For the three newly observed pools, the directly verified revenue element is the pool swap fee; AERO emissions or other rewards are not assumed without pool-specific gauge confirmation.

Limitations

All three newly observed pools are about two days old and therefore have short histories. Current liquidity and volume metrics are snapshots from a single GeckoTerminal surface and were not cross-checked against a second provider at the same timestamp. In concentrated liquidity, displayed liquidity can differ from active in-range liquidity and realized fee accrual. Pool-specific AERO gauges/emissions, Merkl rewards, Beefy vaults and durable APY were not independently confirmed for MSFTc, SNDKc or TSLAc, so this brief does not annualize yield beyond documenting trading-fee settings. Coinbase Tokenized Stocks also carry issuer/custody, redemption, transfer-eligibility and 24/5 underlying-market versus 24/7 DEX price-formation constraints, while LPs face impermanent-loss and range-out risk.
