Aerodrome Tokenized-Stock LP APYs Unwind Rapidly from Four Digits

The extreme APYs previously observed on Aerodrome Slipstream tokenized-stock pools fell sharply in newer data. RenditeMonitor showed AAPLc/USDC at 164.55%, NVDAc/USDC at 467.69% and GOOGLc/USDC at 140.25%, while other DefiLlama surfaces still displayed materially different values.

2026-09-02T11:36:42+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

The prior observation showed AAPLc/USDC at 1,450.92%, NVDAc/USDC at 1,374.37% and GOOGLc/USDC at 954.33%. Newer RenditeMonitor figures fell to 164.55%, 467.69% and 140.25%, respectively. TVL remained around $1.3 million for AAPLc, $1.6 million for NVDAc and $1.4 million for GOOGLc, suggesting that capital inflow and normalization of incentives or fee rates may both have contributed. DefiLlama's NVDAc search result showed 314.43%, including 60.72% base and 253.71% AERO rewards.

Why it matters

The episode reinforces that four-digit tokenized-stock LP APYs were not stable yield levels. They can move sharply with AERO incentives, trading volume and LP capital. The large gaps between data surfaces also make it necessary to separate base fees, rewards, TVL and timestamp rather than compare headline APYs alone.

Limitations

The main limitation is data-update divergence. DefiLlama search results, exact-pool pages and RWA aggregates did not agree for the same pools, and RenditeMonitor also differed. RenditeMonitor did not clearly state the timezone of its update timestamp. APY remains sensitive to AERO allocations, swap volume, active concentrated-liquidity ranges and TVL flows. Tokenized stocks also retain issuer/custody, primary mint/redemption eligibility and off-hours pricing risks.
