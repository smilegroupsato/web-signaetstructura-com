Aerodrome GOOGLc/USDC APY Spikes as TVL Falls About 89%

Aerodrome Slipstream GOOGLc/USDC on Base rose from a prior observed APY of 96.60% to 412.17% while TVL fell from about $1.4 million to $157,000. The displayed yield split was 105.56% base and 306.61% rewards.

2026-09-02T08:41:45+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

Compared with the prior observation, headline APY rose 315.57 percentage points, or about 4.27 times, while TVL fell roughly 88.8%. Base APY increased from 23.76% to 105.56% and reward APY from 72.84% to 306.61%. CoinGecko showed about $3.44 million in 24-hour volume for the Aerodrome GOOGLc/USDC market, large relative to the reduced TVL.

Why it matters

The high APY coincided with lower liquidity, higher fee density and concentrated incentives rather than straightforward capital inflow. Tokenized-stock LP yields therefore need to be read together with TVL and the split between base fees and external rewards.

Limitations

APY and TVL can change sharply, and third-party data surfaces may differ because of update timing and caching. The prior value is an earlier observation of the same public pool and may no longer be reproducible on a live page. Rewards account for most of the displayed APY and can fall if incentives are reduced. Concentrated-liquidity range/IL risk, capacity constraints and tokenized-stock issuer/custody and off-hours pricing risks also remain.
