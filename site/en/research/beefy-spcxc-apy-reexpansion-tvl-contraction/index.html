Beefy USDC-SPCXc displayed APY re-expands to 324% as TVL contracts

On Base, Portals shows Beefy Moo USDC-SPCXc rising from 235.84% APY at 13:08 JST on 2026-09-17 to 323.55% at 17:55 JST, while TVL fell from about $152.9K to $127.7K. That is an 87.71 percentage-point APY increase, or about 37.2%, alongside a roughly $25.2K, or 16.5%, TVL decline. The associated Beefy CLM SPCXc-USDC Reward Pool also moved in the same direction, reaching 159.62% APY with $149.43K TVL.

What changed

At the 2026-09-17 13:08:28 JST Portals observation, Beefy Moo USDC-SPCXc showed 235.84% APY and about $152.9K TVL, while the associated Reward Pool showed 134.08% APY and about $174.49K TVL. By 17:55:39 JST, Moo showed 323.55% and $127.7K, while the Reward Pool showed 159.62% and $149.43K. Moo therefore gained 87.71 percentage points of APY while TVL fell about 16.5%; the Reward Pool gained 25.54 percentage points while TVL fell about 14.4%. Portals also shows seven-day APY changes of +117.30 points for Moo and +48.39 points for the Reward Pool. No new incentive program or increase in the current-epoch reward budget was independently confirmed in this observation.

Why it matters

The same data surface shows APY rising as TVL contracts, which may reflect a fixed or short-duration reward budget being distributed across less liquidity, as well as harvest and compounding recognition or short-window annualization effects. Yield is not primarily dividend carry from the tokenized stock itself; it is composed of concentrated-liquidity trading fees, Aerodrome weekly AERO emissions, USDC incentives distributed through Merkl, Beefy Boosts, and CLM rebalancing and compounding.

Limitations

Portals headline APY may be sensitive to reward recognition, harvest and compounding timing, annualization windows, and data-refresh cadence. This observation did not obtain a synchronized current reading from DefiLlama or DeFi Terminal, and it did not independently verify the SPCXc current-epoch AERO, Merkl, or Boost budgets, remaining campaign duration, or recent realized harvests, so 323.55% should not be treated as persistent realized yield. Risks include concentrated-liquidity range-out and impermanent loss/LVR, Beefy/Aerodrome smart-contract and automation risk, SPCXc issuer/custody/redemption/eligibility risk, and basis effects between 24/5 equity markets and 24/7 Base trading.
