Stockify B20 distributions reach $137K, up 65% in four days

On Base, Stockify has now distributed $137,143.24 of B20 tokenized stocks across 476 settled cycles to 471 eligible holders through a mechanism that converts STFY trading fees into direct stock payouts. The public observation on 2026-09-06 recorded $83,090.74, 388 cycles and 375 eligible holders, implying an increase of about $54,052.50, or 65.1%, in cumulative distributions. The active index remains equally weighted at 20% each across NVDAc, AAPLc, GOOGLc, METAc and SPCXc.

What changed

Since the public observation on 2026-09-06, cumulative B20 distributions rose from $83,090.74 to $137,143.24, a 65.1% increase. Settled cycles increased from 388 to 476, while eligible holders increased from 375 to 471, up 96 or 25.6%. The five-stock index remains unchanged at equal 20% weights.

Why it matters

Stockify does not repackage the underlying companies' dividend yield. Its ETH/STFY Uniswap v4 market charges a 3% hook fee, of which 90% is routed into B20 stock purchases and then pushed directly to qualifying holders. Growth in cumulative distributions and eligible holders shows that the composition converting token trading fees into a tokenized-stock basket is continuing to operate at meaningful scale.

Limitations

The comparison baseline is the public observation from 2026-09-06, not a one-hour change. Stockify does not publish an estimated APY or simulated yield history, and distributions depend on STFY trading volume. The contracts are unaudited, while the owner can change the eligibility threshold, reward exclusions, index composition and weights between cycles, and the swap-venue allowlist. B20 assets may impose their own transfer policies and jurisdictional eligibility requirements. The Stockify site footer also still states 'Pre-launch. No STFY market is live.', which conflicts with the live protocol documentation and distribution data shown elsewhere on the site.
