Euler V2 balances observed across four Coinbase B20 stocks on Base

DefiLlama asset pages for Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base show Euler V2 DeFi Active TVL of $1,236 for NVDAc, $936 for METAc, $863 for GOOGLc, and $738 for AAPLc, or about $3,773 combined. Base's official stock ecosystem page describes Euler as modular lending and borrowing for tokenized stocks. The footprint remains very small, and the exact vault configuration, utilization, supply/borrow APYs, and any B20-specific incentives were not independently verified.

What changed

Current DefiLlama asset pages display distinct Euler V2 balances across all four of NVDAc, METAc, GOOGLc, and AAPLc: $1,236, $936, $863, and $738 respectively, about $3,773 combined. This makes a lending-protocol balance footprint observable alongside the AMM and yield-vault routes already visible for tokenized stocks.

Why it matters

The data provides a concrete protocol-balance signal that tokenized stocks on Base are reaching a lending/collateral layer, rather than only appearing in trading and vault compositions. At roughly $3.8K combined, however, the scale is closer to a pilot or test footprint and does not by itself demonstrate sustained borrowing demand, durable lending yield, or an incentive program.

Limitations

DefiLlama's asset-level DeFi Active TVL shows balances attributed to Euler V2, but it does not establish from this view whether each amount is lend supply, posted collateral, or another vault configuration; the exact market address, collateral/debt pair, oracle, LTV, and liquidation parameters were not verified. B20-specific supply/borrow APYs, utilization, and additional rewards or incentives were also not independently verified. The four asset pages should not be treated as a perfectly synchronized snapshot; the AAPLc page had an older crawl timestamp in the search layer than the other three. No second data provider was found in this run that reproduced the Euler balances at the same per-asset granularity. Combined balances are only about $3.8K, and tokenized-stock issuer, custody, redemption, jurisdictional, and 24/5-underlying-versus-24/7-DeFi limitations remain.
