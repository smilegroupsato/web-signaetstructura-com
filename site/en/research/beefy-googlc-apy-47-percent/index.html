Beefy USDC–GOOGLc Shows 47.09% APY on DefiLlama

DefiLlama showed Beefy USDC–GOOGLc on Base at 47.09% APY with about $284,000 in TVL. The yield depends on Aerodrome liquidity provision plus external incentives rather than swap fees alone.

2026-09-02T09:26:00+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

DefiLlama showed mooBeefyAerodromeBaseUSDC-GOOGLc at 47.09% Supply APY and $284,394 in TVL. Beefy's official vault page confirms that the GOOGLc-USDC CLM deposits GOOGLc and USDC into Aerodrome concentrated liquidity on Base and earns swap rewards.

Why it matters

Yield on Base tokenized-stock liquidity is influenced by more than organic trading fees. Beefy describes a stack of Aerodrome emissions, USDC incentives distributed through Merkl and Beefy boosts, so the headline APY should not be read as pure swap-fee yield.

Limitations

APY can move with incentive levels, update timing and aggregation methods. Concentrated-liquidity range risk, impermanent loss, incentive reductions, issuer/custody risk for tokenized stocks and off-hours price dislocations versus the underlying equity remain material limitations.
