Market Watch · Base Tokenized Stock Yield Watch

Zyfai layers 10% USDC and rZFI rewards onto an automated Base tokenized-stock allocator

Zyfai is promoting Yield Accounts for NVDAc, AAPLc, METAc and GOOGLc on Base that route into underlying opportunities via Superform and IPOR and add Zyfai incentives on top. Its public figures show 9-24% underlying pool yield including pool rewards, an additional 10% APY in USDC incentives and 1.68% APY in rZFI rewards, for a headline total of up to 35% APY. The Yield Account can rebalance between venues, making this a meta-allocation layer rather than a single vault.

What changed

This observation captures a new incentive stack in which Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are allocated into existing Superform/IPOR yield venues while Zyfai adds its own USDC and rZFI rewards. Zyfai is also promoting a Swap flow in which the Yield Account handles withdrawal, swapping and redepositing into the selected opportunity for another supported asset, extending tokenized-stock yield into an automated cross-venue reallocation composition.

Why it matters

Base tokenized-stock yield is expanding beyond individual vaults and LPs into higher-level allocators that compare and move capital among underlying venues. The headline yield is not purely equity-derived: it combines 9-24% underlying pool yield with a 10% USDC incentive and 1.68% rZFI reward, so underlying return and temporary incentive layers need to be evaluated separately.

Limitations

The up-to-35% APY is a Zyfai-disclosed headline. This observation could not independently verify current APY by individual stock, tokenized-stock-specific TVL, the total budget, duration or wallet cap of the additional USDC incentive, or the vesting terms and realized value of rZFI. Underlying yield includes rewards at Superform/IPOR venues and can change as the allocator rebalances. Additional risks and limitations include the Zyfai smart-account, agent and routing layer, underlying protocols, incentive expiry, and B20 issuer, custody, redemption and eligible-jurisdiction constraints. Displayed APY does not guarantee future realized yield.
