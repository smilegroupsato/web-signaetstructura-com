Aerodrome GOOGLc/WETH displayed APY compresses from 196% to 124% in roughly two hours

On Base, DefiLlama's displayed APY for the Aerodrome Slipstream GOOGLc/WETH pool fell from 195.80% at 2026-09-07 10:47 JST to 124.39% at 13:02 JST. TVL declined only about 1.8%, from $151,089 to $148,422, while the displayed components both contracted: base from 92.24% to 66.35% and AERO reward from 103.56% to 58.04%.

2026-09-07T13:02:01+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

Total displayed APY fell 71.41 percentage points, from 195.80% to 124.39%, a relative decline of about 36.5%. The base component fell 25.89 points from 92.24% to 66.35%, while the AERO reward component fell 45.52 points from 103.56% to 58.04%. TVL decreased by only $2,667, or about 1.8%, so the APY move was observed as a simultaneous compression in short-term fee density and the displayed reward component rather than as a large capital outflow.

Why it matters

The move shows how quickly headline yield can change in a tokenized-stock/WETH LP even when capital remains broadly stable. Aerodrome's official documentation states that LPs either earn AERO emissions when staked or swap fees when unstaked; a single deposit does not earn both simultaneously. DefiLlama's base + reward display should therefore be treated as an observation of market activity and incentive conditions rather than as a guaranteed realized yield for one position.

Limitations

DefiLlama's headline APY annualizes short-term data and does not guarantee future or realized yield for a single position. Aerodrome's official design separates staked AERO emissions from unstaked swap-fee earnings, so DefiLlama's combined base + reward presentation may not map directly to one executable LP configuration. This comparison uses point-in-time DefiLlama snapshots and was not independently verified with a real-time gauge-emission read or realized LP PnL. Concentrated liquidity carries range-out, impermanent-loss and LVR risk; GOOGLc/WETH also combines equity-token and ETH price exposure. AERO allocation can change each epoch, while tokenized stocks add issuer, custody, mint/redemption, eligibility and transfer constraints, plus timing mismatches between a 24/5 reference equity market and a 24/7 DEX.
