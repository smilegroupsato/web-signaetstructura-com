Aerodrome GOOGLc/WETH displayed APY re-expands from 97% to 196% with TVL nearly flat

On Base, DefiLlama's RWA surface shows Aerodrome Slipstream GOOGLc/WETH at $151,089 TVL and 195.80% APY, split into 92.24% base yield and 103.56% AERO rewards. The prior observation at 2026-09-06 23:51:27 JST showed $148,085 TVL and 97.01% APY, split into 38.80% base and 58.21% rewards. What changed

Total APY moved from 97.01% to 195.80%, up 98.79 percentage points or about 101.8% on a relative basis. The swap-driven base component rose from 38.80% to 92.24%, up 53.44 points, while the AERO reward component increased from 58.21% to 103.56%, up 45.35 points. TVL rose from $148,085 to $151,089, an increase of $3,004 or about 2.0%, so the yield increase is not explained solely by a sharp contraction in reported TVL. The current public surface shows both fee-derived yield and AERO incentives re-expanding at the same time.

Why it matters

This tokenized-stock/WETH LP is maintaining roughly the same capital scale while both swap-fee yield and AERO emissions strengthen. It adds evidence that tokenized-stock DeFi activity on Base is extending beyond USDC pairs into direct crypto-denominated LPs. The headline APY remains highly sensitive to short-term trading activity, active liquidity and AERO allocation, and should not be interpreted as equity dividend yield.

Limitations

DefiLlama APY is an annualized display derived from short-horizon swap activity, active liquidity, TVL and AERO emissions and does not guarantee future realized returns. GeckoTerminal search retrieval still exposes an older launch-stage snapshot, highlighting material cache and timing differences across providers. Concentrated liquidity introduces range-out risk, impermanent loss and LVR; GOOGLc/WETH also carries both equity-linked and ETH price volatility. AERO rewards can fall or end as epoch voting and allocation change. Tokenized stocks also carry issuer, custody, mint/redemption, eligibility and transfer constraints, plus timing gaps between 24/5 reference equity markets and a 24/7 DEX.
