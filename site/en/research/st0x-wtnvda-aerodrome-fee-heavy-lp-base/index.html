A Fee-Heavy Aerodrome LP for ST0x's Tokenized NVIDIA Asset Emerges on Base

An Aerodrome Slipstream USDC/wtNVDA pool adds a parallel ST0x tokenized-equity lane on Base. DeFi Terminal showed 86.26% APY, split into 57.65% base and 28.61% rewards, with about $31,800 TVL. DefiLlama showed 131.37% APY and about $28,642 TVL, highlighting a material refresh or aggregation gap.

2026-09-02T21:26:21+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

Previous observations in this series concentrated on Coinbase-issued NVDAc, AAPLc, METAc and GOOGLc across Superform, TAU, Clearstar, Beefy and Aerodrome. The newly captured USDC/wtNVDA pool adds a parallel ST0x tokenized-equity lane on Base. This should not be read as evidence that the pool launched on 2026-09-02; rather, it is a newly discovered market that had not previously been included in this watch. In the DeFi Terminal snapshot, roughly two-thirds of the displayed APY came from base yield associated with pool trading economics rather than token rewards, making the displayed composition more fee-heavy than many of the Coinbase cAsset Aerodrome pools recently observed in this series.

Why it matters

Tokenized-stock activity on Base is extending beyond the Coinbase B20 stack into a separate ST0x issuer ecosystem with liquidity, yield and broader composability. ST0x describes wtNVDA as a Base vault wrapper around tokenized NVIDIA exposure. Public vvveity documentation also shows ST0x stock tokens being used as quote assets for stock-paired launches, and states that wtCOIN, wtMSTR and wtSPYM can be used as collateral on Euler and can earn HYDX rewards on Hydrex. Together, these markets point to a parallel tokenized-equity stack in which equity-linked assets are moving beyond passive holdings into LPs, quote assets and collateral. The USDC/wtNVDA pool itself remains small, however, at roughly $30,000 in TVL.

Limitations

DeFi Terminal uses DefiLlama-sourced data, so the difference between 86.26% and 131.37% should not be treated as two independent onchain measurements; it is likely driven by refresh timing, caching or aggregation differences. APY is a variable annualization of recent swap-fee economics and AERO rewards and does not establish persistence. The USDC/wtNVDA pool has only about $30,000 in TVL, creating meaningful capacity and liquidity constraints alongside concentrated-liquidity range risk, impermanent loss, slippage, reward changes and smart-contract risk. DefiLlama notes that it could not identify a public reserve attestation for wtNVDA, while ST0x's legal structure treats token holders as contractual creditors of the issuer rather than direct shareholders. The Euler collateral and Hydrex reward references in vvveity documentation describe broader ST0x ecosystem composability and do not represent additional yield generated by the USDC/wtNVDA pool itself.
