Beefy MSTRc strategy on Base shows a sharp APY and TVL data-provider divergence

Data-provider readings for the USDC-MSTRc Beefy strategy on Base have diverged sharply. The Moo strategy is shown at 29.07% APY on DefiLlama versus 285.46% on Portals, a roughly 9.8x gap. The Reward strategy is aligned near 149.36% APY, but TVL differs by about 8.2x: roughly $28.59k on DefiLlama versus $233.18k on Portals.

What changed

At the previous observation, the Moo strategy was closely aligned at 177.97% APY on DefiLlama and 182.54% on Portals. In this observation, DefiLlama fell to 29.07% while Portals shows 285.46%. For the Reward strategy, APY remains aligned at 149.37% on Portals and 149.36% on DefiLlama, while TVL differs substantially. Upstream Aerodrome Slipstream USDC-MSTRc is now at 3,488.28% APY, down from 4,655.41% at the prior observation.

Why it matters

When APY and TVL readings diverge this widely for the same or closely related strategies, differences in AERO emissions, harvest/update cadence, annualization methods, and CLM accounting scope can materially reduce the comparability of headline APY. The yield source remains concentrated-LP swap fees plus AERO incentives, with Beefy adding automated compounding and CLM management.
