SPCXc/USDC liquidity fragments as a 5% fee pool forms on Uniswap V4

A new 5% fee SPCXc/USDC pool is visible on Uniswap V4 on Base alongside the existing Aerodrome market. At observation time, the Uniswap V4 pool showed $18,860.87 of liquidity, $25,898.56 of 24-hour volume, 284 transactions and an age of roughly six hours. On the same GeckoTerminal surface, the related Aerodrome SPCXc/USDC market showed roughly $11,420 of liquidity and $380,960 of 24-hour volume, materially below the $44,954.13 liquidity recorded for Aerodrome at 03:43 JST on 2026-09-05.

What changed

At 03:43 JST on 2026-09-05, the Aerodrome SPCXc/USDC observation showed $44,954.13 of liquidity and $225,747.04 of 24-hour volume. A Uniswap V4 SPCXc/USDC pool with a 5% trading fee has since been identified at 0x65206fdc2323af155a01c9be845fa500e6d1058c207d2f2d83e9c48b706d0170, showing $18,860.87 of liquidity, $25,898.56 of 24-hour volume, 284 transactions and an age of roughly six hours. The related-pool view on GeckoTerminal currently shows Aerodrome SPCXc/USDC at about $11,420 of liquidity and $380,960 of 24-hour volume. The displayed Aerodrome liquidity is about 74.6% below the 03:43 observation while displayed volume is about 68.8% higher.

Why it matters

The verified yield source for the new Uniswap V4 pool is its 5% swap fee; no external reward, additional incentive or sustainable APY was independently confirmed at observation time. The change shows SPCXc liquidity beginning to split across venues and fee tiers. A 5% trading fee may also increase adverse-selection risk, and volume from a pool only about six hours old should not be annualized into a durable yield estimate. Coinbase states that Tokenized Stock dividends and splits are reflected through an onchain multiplier rather than token-balance changes, which is also relevant when comparing raw token-price displays across venues and indexers.

Limitations

The Uniswap V4 pool is only about six hours old, so its 5% fee cannot be converted directly into realized LP returns or a sustainable APY. Active liquidity, LP range, fee growth, hook details, protocol fees and external rewards or incentives were not independently verified in this observation. The current Aerodrome figure comes from GeckoTerminal's related-pool view and is not a synchronized point-in-time comparison with the 03:43 baseline. Concentrated-liquidity range risk, impermanent loss, SPCXc issuer/custody/redemption/eligibility constraints, differences in how venues and indexers reflect the B20 multiplier, and the gap between round-the-clock onchain trading and reference-asset price formation remain relevant limitations.
