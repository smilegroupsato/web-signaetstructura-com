Market Watch · Base Tokenized Stock Yield Watch

Base tokenized-stock DEX volume is reported at a $100M daily record as NVDAc LP fee APY rebounds

At the 2026-09-13 13:14 JST observation, Aerodrome Slipstream USDC/NVDAc on Base showed about $2.65M TVL, 90.04% total APY, 40.67% base APY, and 49.37% AERO reward APY. Versus the 06:12 JST observation, TVL rose only about 0.8%, while total APY increased about 55.4%, base APY about 172.3%, and AERO reward APY about 14.8%. The latest rebound is driven more by the fee-derived base component than by rewards.

What changed

Aerodrome USDC/NVDAc moved from about $2.63M TVL, 57.94% total APY, 14.93% base APY, and 43.01% AERO reward APY at 06:12 JST to about $2.65M, 90.04%, 40.67%, and 49.37% at 13:14 JST. TVL was nearly unchanged while base APY rose to roughly 2.7x its earlier level. On the same date, Crypto Briefing, citing Token Terminal data, reported that Base tokenized stocks reached a record $100M in daily DEX volume. That market-wide volume figure is secondary public reporting; this observation did not directly access Token Terminal's primary dashboard.

Why it matters

Unlike the recent APY swings that were dominated by AERO emissions, this move is led by a sharp increase in the fee-derived base APY of the concentrated-liquidity position. Together with reported growth in tokenized-stock DEX activity across Base, it may indicate stronger transaction-flow monetization for tokenized stocks used as LP assets. However, short-window volume annualization can make base APY appear unusually high, so persistence remains the key limitation.

Limitations

APY can change rapidly with short-window swap volume, concentrated-liquidity ranges, and AERO gauge or emission allocation, and it does not guarantee future realized yield. Concentrated LPs carry IL/LVR, range-out, and slippage risk. NVDAc carries issuer, custody, redemption, eligibility, and off-hours basis risks. Data providers may differ in update timing and methodology. The $100M daily DEX-volume figure comes from secondary public reporting citing Token Terminal; the primary dashboard was not directly accessed in this observation.
