SPCXc AERO rewards surge on Aerodrome and propagate into Beefy vault yields

On Base, displayed yield for the Aerodrome Slipstream USDC/SPCXc pool rose sharply while TVL remained broadly stable, driven mainly by AERO rewards. The repricing also propagated into Beefy's Moo vault and Reward Cow built on the same liquidity venue.

2026-09-15T14:08:47+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

The Aerodrome direct pool page showed TVL of $936,153 and APY of 837.21% (52.94% base and 784.27% AERO reward). The preceding search-index snapshot showed TVL of $920,522 and APY of 156.68% (50.89% base and 105.79% reward), implying roughly +1.7% TVL but +680.53 percentage points in APY, or about 5.34x. Beefy Moo showed TVL of $144,237 and APY of 202.80%, up 162.73 points from a 40.07% indexed snapshot. Reward Cow showed TVL of $82,711 and APY of 122.60% (56.87% base and 65.73% reward), up 85.36 points from 37.24% previously.

Why it matters

The move appears to be driven primarily by AERO emissions rather than by a large TVL change, showing how incentive repricing on a tokenized-stock LP can quickly propagate into Beefy's auto-compounding layer. However, DefiLlama itself showed 837.21% on the Aerodrome direct pool page versus 1,778.21% on the SPCXc aggregation page, a roughly 2.12x headline-APY gap that highlights differences in annualization, update timing or aggregation surfaces.

Limitations

Displayed yield depends on concentrated-liquidity swap fees and AERO emissions on Aerodrome, plus auto-compounding and range management on Beefy, and can change abruptly with emissions, volume and prices. DefiLlama's direct pool and RWA aggregation surfaces showed large differences in APY and TVL, so they should not be treated as a single synchronized effective yield. Change calculations use prior search-index snapshots with coarse freshness rather than a fully synchronized historical series. Risks include concentrated-liquidity IL/LVR and range-out, Aerodrome and Beefy smart-contract exposure, and SPCXc issuer, custody, redemption, eligibility and reference-price formation constraints.
