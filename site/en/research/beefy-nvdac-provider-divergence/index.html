Beefy USDC-NVDAc APY diverges: 67% on Portals versus 37% on DefiLlama

For Beefy's USDC-NVDAc vault on Base, Portals displayed 67.26% APY and $334,024.60 TVL, while DefiLlama displayed 36.85% APY and $283,765 TVL. The same vault therefore showed a 30.41 percentage-point APY gap and roughly a $50,260 TVL gap across data providers.

What changed

Portals showed $54,936.18 of 24-hour volume, with $52,987.20 of deposits and $1,948.98 of withdrawals, implying roughly $51,038 of net inflow. At the same observation, headline APY differed by 30.41 percentage points and TVL by about 17.7% between Portals and DefiLlama.

Why it matters

Beefy's published Coinbase Tokenized Stocks strategy combines Aerodrome emissions, Coinbase-funded Merkl USDC incentives and Beefy mooBIFI boosts. With headline APY differing by roughly 1.8x across aggregators, yield-source treatment, compounding, measurement windows and update timing need to be separated when interpreting the displayed return.

Limitations

Portals and DefiLlama may use different APY windows, reward and compounding treatment, and TVL update times, so this is not a perfectly synchronized like-for-like measurement. The current headline APY could not be independently decomposed into swap fees, AERO, Merkl USDC and mooBIFI contributions. Other limitations include concentrated-liquidity range risk, IL/LVR, gauge or incentive changes, vault/strategy contract risk, tokenized-stock issuer, custody, redemption and eligibility constraints, and the timing mismatch between 24/5 underlying equity markets and 24/7 Base trading.
