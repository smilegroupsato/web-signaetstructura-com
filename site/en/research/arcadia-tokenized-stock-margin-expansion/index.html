Arcadia expands Base tokenized stocks into leveraged LP strategies

Arcadia Finance has publicly highlighted new tokenized-stock pools powered by Aerodrome and Base for AMZNc, MSFTc, TSLAc, MSTRc, SNDKc, SPCXc and MAG7. Arcadia can place supported LPs inside margin accounts, combining borrowed capital with concentrated liquidity, auto-compounding and automated rebalancing.

2026-09-15T16:31:42+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

Base's second-wave tokenized stocks had mainly been observed in direct Aerodrome LPs and vault layers such as Beefy. Arcadia's public surface now groups multiple names into a margin-enabled LP composition. Arcadia's documentation defines the Farm APY range as base APY through max leveraged APY using seven-day pool history, so the upper bound is not the simple unlevered LP yield. The public announcement did not provide enough data to establish current TVL, per-pool APY or incremental incentive amounts for each newly listed pool.

Why it matters

The tokenized-stock DeFi stack is extending from spot LP and auto-compounding vaults into collateralized borrowing and leveraged concentrated-liquidity positions. Arcadia documents trading fees as the yield source for non-staked LP positions and token emissions for staked Aerodrome positions; a margin position must also absorb borrowing costs. Max leveraged APY therefore depends on fees or emissions, borrowing rates and range configuration rather than representing a single persistent stock-yield rate.

Limitations

The new-pool announcement was observed from a searchable public mirror of Arcadia's official account; an exact post URL was not captured in this observation. Arcadia's dynamic Farm interface did not yield a reliable same-time snapshot for TVL, APY, borrowing rates or incremental incentives for each new pool, so those figures are omitted. Leveraged concentrated liquidity carries impermanent-loss, LVR, range-out, borrowing-rate, liquidation and smart-contract/automation risk. Tokenized stocks also carry issuer, custody, redemption and eligibility constraints, plus pricing-basis risk between 24/5 equity markets and 24/7 onchain trading.
