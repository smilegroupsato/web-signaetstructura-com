Aerodrome GOOGLc/WETH displayed APY rebounds from 124% to 226%, highlighting rapid yield whipsaw

On Base, DefiLlama's exact-pool page for Aerodrome Slipstream GOOGLc/WETH displayed $151,307 TVL and 226.46% APY at 2026-09-07 14:19 JST, split into 91.55% base and 134.92% AERO reward. At 13:02 JST the same opportunity had been recorded at $148,422 TVL and 124.39% APY, split into 66.35% base and 58.04% reward. TVL rose only about 1.9% while displayed APY increased 102.07 percentage points, or about 82.1% relative.

What changed

From 13:02 JST to 14:19 JST, total displayed APY rose from 124.39% to 226.46%, up 102.07 percentage points. The base component increased 25.20 points from 66.35% to 91.55%, while the AERO reward component increased 76.88 points from 58.04% to 134.92%. TVL increased by $2,885, or about 1.9%, from $148,422 to $151,307. The same pool was displayed at 195.80% at 10:47 JST, 124.39% at 13:02 JST and 226.46% at 14:19 JST, showing a large intraday whipsaw in headline yield while capital remained broadly around $150K.

Why it matters

The observation shows how quickly annualized fee and reward displays can change in a direct tokenized-stock/WETH LP even when TVL is comparatively stable. Aerodrome's official documentation states that LPs earn AERO emissions when staked or swap fees when unstaked, so a base + reward aggregate should not be treated as a guaranteed yield that one position can realize simultaneously. The more useful signal is the rapid variation in the reward layer and aggregation surfaces rather than the headline APY alone.

Limitations

DefiLlama's headline APY annualizes short-term data and does not guarantee future or realized yield for a single position. Aerodrome's official design separates staked AERO emissions from unstaked swap-fee earnings, so the combined base + reward display may not map directly to one executable LP configuration. The exact-pool page and the RWA listing showed different snapshots during this observation, indicating cache or update-timing differences. Real-time gauge emissions and realized LP PnL were not independently verified. Concentrated liquidity carries range-out, impermanent-loss and LVR risk; GOOGLc/WETH also combines equity-token and ETH price exposure. Tokenized stocks add issuer, custody, mint/redemption, eligibility and transfer constraints, plus timing mismatches between a 24/5 reference equity market and a 24/7 DEX.
