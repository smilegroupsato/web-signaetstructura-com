Market Watch · Base Tokenized Stock Yield Watch

Displayed APY on LAPTOP/AAPLc drops about 59% in roughly three hours as TVL rises

On Base, Aerodrome Slipstream's LAPTOP/AAPLc pool has moved from a previously observed displayed APY of 29,228.84% to 12,041.12% on DefiLlama's current AAPLc RWA aggregation surface. TVL increased from $25,854 to $28,478, or about 10.2%. The current displayed APY is split between 2,615.96% base APY and 9,425.16% in AERO rewards, leaving the headline yield still heavily incentive-driven.

2026-09-12T13:30:20+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

At the 2026-09-12 10:39 JST observation, the pool showed 29,228.84% supply APY, 3,340.42% base APY, 25,888.42% AERO reward APY and $25,854 TVL. At the 2026-09-12 13:30 JST observation, DefiLlama's AAPLc RWA aggregation surface showed 12,041.12% supply APY, 2,615.96% base APY, 9,425.16% AERO reward APY and $28,478 TVL. Total APY fell about 58.8%, the base component fell about 21.7%, and the AERO reward component fell about 63.6%, while TVL rose about 10.2%. AERO rewards declined from about 88.6% to about 78.3% of displayed APY.

Why it matters

The yield source is not equity carry or dividends from the tokenized stock itself. It is a combination of annualized concentrated-liquidity trading fees and AERO incentives. The move shows how launch-stage headline yields on tokenized-stock LPs can compress within hours as liquidity, fee flow and emissions conditions change, making APY decomposition and observation timing essential context.

Limitations

LAPTOP/AAPLc is a very young, high-volatility concentrated-liquidity pool. Both the base APY, which annualizes short-window fee activity, and AERO emissions can change rapidly. DefiLlama's RWA aggregation surface and exact-pool search retrieval were not synchronized, so the displayed figures should not be treated as a single definitive same-second snapshot. LPs face impermanent loss, LVR, range-out and incentive-decay risk. AAPLc also carries tokenized-stock-specific issuer/custody, mint/redemption eligibility and off-hours price-dislocation limitations.
