TAU Stock Carry Vaults activate Morpho/Steakhouse carry on Base

TAU Labs announced that its Stock Carry Vaults on Base have activated carry strategies that borrow USDC against Coinbase Tokenized Stocks through Morpho markets curated by SteakhouseFi. DefiLlama currently shows AAPLCT at $38,186 TVL and 10.27% APY, split into 0.27% base and 10.00% reward APY.

2026-09-17T07:14:17+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

The indexed IPOR Fusion Nvidia Carry Trade page had still described the vault as being in a pre-deposit phase ahead of yield-generation ignition. TAU Labs now says the carry strategies are activated. AAPLCT, previously shown around a 10.00% reward-only target, now displays 10.27% APY with 0.27% base APY, while TVL is roughly unchanged from about $38.16K to $38.19K. The Morpho Blue AAPLc/USDC market shows a 6.61% borrow APY, $478,965 available liquidity, and a 62.5% LTV.

Why it matters

The material change is not a TVL spike but the transition of the tokenized-stock-collateral-to-USDC-borrow carry engine into active operation. At the observation time, most of AAPLCT's headline APY still comes from the 10% reward component and the displayed base carry remains small.

Limitations

DefiLlama shows 10.00 percentage points of AAPLCT's 10.27% APY as rewards, with only 0.27% as base APY. Borrow costs are variable and can compress or invert the carry spread. The structure introduces smart-contract, oracle and liquidation risks across Fusion, Morpho, the curator and downstream deployment venues, plus tokenized-stock issuer, custody, redemption and eligibility risks and the timing mismatch between 24/5 equity markets and 24/7 Base markets. Public IPOR pages and aggregators may update at different times, and equivalent base-carry activation for every TAU stock vault was not independently verified at this observation. CURATED separately announced an extra 10% APR in USDC and 20% more Fusion Points, but whether that incentive is included in the 10.27% figure, along with its duration and cap, was not verified.
