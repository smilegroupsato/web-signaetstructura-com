Aerodrome Tokenized-Stock LP Rewards Re-Expand Across AAPLc, METAc and GOOGLc

Displayed yields on Aerodrome Slipstream tokenized-stock/USDC pools expanded again. AAPLc showed 375.31% APY with about $1.16 million TVL, METAc 657.17% with about $946,000 TVL and GOOGLc 349.84% with about $1.32 million TVL, with rewards accounting for most of the increase.

2026-09-02T17:33:26+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

The prior patrol observed AAPLc/USDC at 231.48% APY, METAc/USDC at 234.62% and GOOGLc/USDC at 96.60%. The new observation showed AAPLc at 375.31%, including 64.96% base and 310.35% AERO rewards; METAc at 657.17%, including 73.96% base and 583.20% rewards; and GOOGLc at 349.84%, including 48.81% base and 301.03% rewards. TVL remained broadly around the $1 million level across these pools, so the move is difficult to explain by TVL collapse alone.

Why it matters

AERO incentives are moving rapidly across several tokenized-stock LPs and can multiply headline APY without a comparable change in pool capital. The latest re-expansion was driven more by rewards than base fees, reinforcing the need to separate underlying trading activity from external incentives and to account for asynchronous updates across data surfaces.

Limitations

Displayed APY is an annualized variable that can move sharply with swap volume, active concentrated-liquidity ranges, TVL and AERO incentive allocation. DefiLlama exact-pool pages, RWA aggregates and DeFi Terminal were not synchronized, so these figures should be treated as observation-time displays. Tokenized stocks retain issuer/custody, eligibility, mint/redemption and off-hours pricing risks, while concentrated LPs add range and impermanent-loss risk.
