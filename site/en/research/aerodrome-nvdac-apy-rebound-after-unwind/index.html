Aerodrome NVDAc LP Rebounds Above 300% APY After a Sharp Drop

Aerodrome Slipstream USDC-NVDAc on Base rebounded from roughly 58% APY in the prior patrol to above 300%. DeFi Terminal showed 351.08% and DefiLlama 314.43%, while TVL fell from about $2.0 million to roughly $1.65-$1.70 million.

2026-09-02T14:44:29+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

The prior patrol observed USDC-NVDAc at 58.36% APY and about $2.0 million TVL. DeFi Terminal later showed 351.08% APY, split into 68.91% base and 282.17% rewards, with about $1.70 million TVL. DefiLlama showed 314.43%, split into 60.72% base and 253.71% AERO rewards, with about $1.65 million TVL. Headline APY therefore rebounded about 5.4-6.0 times while TVL declined roughly 15-17.5%.

Why it matters

A tokenized-stock LP moving from tens of percent to above 300% within hours shows why headline APY cannot be treated as a persistent yield level. NVDAc remained reward-heavy, but base fees also recovered into the 60% range, so organic trading activity and incentives need to be separated. The roughly 37-point gap between data providers also makes timestamp and aggregation path important.

Limitations

APY is an annualized variable that can move sharply with swap volume, active concentrated-liquidity ranges, TVL and AERO reward allocation. DeFi Terminal and DefiLlama were not fully synchronized and showed 351.08% versus 314.43%. The prior 58.36% figure is a patrol-time observation and may not remain reproducible on a live page. Tokenized stocks also carry issuer/custody, eligibility, mint/redemption and off-hours pricing risks.
