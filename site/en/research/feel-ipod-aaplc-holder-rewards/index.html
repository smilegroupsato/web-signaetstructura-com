Feel.cash Adds Daily AAPLc Rewards for IPOD Holders

Feel.cash has introduced daily Apple stock-token rewards for holders of IPOD, an IPOD/AAPLc market on Base, and says the first $2.28K has already been distributed. No staking or claim step is required, adding a direct stock-token holder incentive on top of an existing stock-paired liquidity structure.

2026-09-11T21:16:09+09:00base-tokenized-stock-yield-watch

What changed

IPOD was already directly paired with AAPLc, but on 2026-09-11 Feel.cash designated IPOD as its flagship and added daily AAPLc distributions for simply holding IPOD. The official post says the first $2.28K has already been distributed. Asynchronous GeckoTerminal snapshots for the same pool show roughly $143K-$336K of liquidity, $182K-$251K of 24-hour volume, and about 1.4K-1.5K holders.

Why it matters

The composition now uses a tokenized stock in two roles: as the quote/liquidity asset for a speculative token market and as a direct reward distributed to holders of that speculative token. The reward has not been established as Apple equity dividend income and should currently be treated as an external incentive.

Evidence

  • Feel.cash official public feed — Feel.cash says IPOD is its flagship, that IPOD holders now receive Apple stock every day, and that the first $2.28K has already been distributed. 2026-09-11T21:16:09+09:00
  • IPOD official site — The official IPOD site states that IPOD is paired with AAPLc on Base. 2026-09-11T21:16:09+09:00
  • GeckoTerminal IPOD/AAPL pool — Snapshots for the same pool taken from localized/cached views show approximately $143K-$336K in liquidity, $182K-$251K in 24-hour volume, and about 1.4K-1.5K holders, indicating substantial timing differences across snapshots. 2026-09-11T21:16:09+09:00

Limitations

Public materials reviewed do not disclose the AAPLc reward funding source, daily budget, distribution formula, snapshot or eligibility logic, end date, or APY. The $2.28K figure is an initial distribution amount, not evidence of a durable annualized yield. GeckoTerminal localized snapshots differ materially because of timing/cache effects, so no single current liquidity or valuation figure is asserted. IPOD is a highly volatile speculative token, while AAPLc carries issuer, custody, redemption, and eligibility constraints.
