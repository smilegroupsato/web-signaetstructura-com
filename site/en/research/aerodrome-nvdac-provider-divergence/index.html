Aerodrome NVDAc/USDC displayed APY diverges sharply between 2,293% and 165%

For Aerodrome Slipstream USDC-NVDAc on Base, DefiLlama's live pool page displayed 2,293.52% APY, split into 81.30% base and 2,212.22% AERO rewards, with about $2.04M TVL. DeFi Terminal displayed the same pool at 164.68% APY, split into 34.11% base and 130.57% rewards, with about $2.4M TVL. GeckoTerminal showed roughly $2.03M of liquidity and $8.36M of 24-hour volume for the same pool address, placing its liquidity reading closer to DefiLlama's live page.

What changed

In this observation, the displayed APY gap across data surfaces for the same Aerodrome USDC-NVDAc pool widened to 2,128.84 percentage points. On DefiLlama's live page, roughly 96.5% of the displayed yield came from the AERO reward component, while DeFi Terminal showed only 130.57% reward APY. TVL also differed at about $2.04M versus $2.4M.

Why it matters

The pool itself has more than $2M of liquidity and substantial trading activity, but the headline annualized yield changes by an order of magnitude depending on the data surface. Evaluating tokenized-stock LP yield therefore requires separating AERO allocation, short-window annualization, update timing and aggregation methodology rather than treating a single headline APY as a stable return estimate.

Limitations

This is a point-in-time comparison of displayed metrics. The underlying AERO gauge allocation for the current epoch and realized LP returns were not independently read from the gauge. DefiLlama's combined base-plus-reward presentation may not map to one executable Aerodrome LP position because Aerodrome separates fee earnings from staked AERO emissions. APY can change rapidly with short-window volume, prices, reward allocation, TVL and update timing. Concentrated liquidity carries range-out and impermanent-loss/LVR risk, while NVDAc adds issuer, custody, redemption and eligibility constraints plus a 24/5 underlying-equity versus 24/7 Base trading mismatch.
