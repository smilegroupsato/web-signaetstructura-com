Methods

Observation, reconstruction and falsifiability.

How SIGNA ET STRUCTURA handles evidence, numerical reconstruction, historical replay and social/on-chain analysis.

Evidence

We prioritize primary sources, attach acquisition timing to important claims, and separate facts, hypotheses, opinions and forecasts.

Historical Replay

Replay uses only information available as of the replay date. Future price action is revealed only after the selection and evaluation step. False positives and false negatives are retained.

Figures

Where possible, Data Figures retain source data and figure specifications so SVG and PNG outputs can be reproduced. Observed, reconstructed, estimated, interpolated and unavailable values are distinguished.

Limitations

Missing data, provider differences, liquidity constraints, inaccessible sources and estimates are disclosed where material.
