Base Tokenized Stock Yield Watch

A continuing watch of material structural changes in tokenized-stock yield markets on Base.

What we watch

We track new vaults and protocols, material TVL shifts, incentives, changes in yield composition, and new uses of tokenized stocks as collateral, LP assets or structured products. No material change means no post.

Market Watch · 2026-09-02

NVDAc Emerges as a Quote Asset for a Meme Token Market on Base

A Uniswap V4 market on Base pairs BLUECHIP directly with NVDAc, Coinbase's tokenized NVIDIA stock. BLUECHIP's official site identifies the BLUECHIP/NVDAc pair on o1 Launchpad, while a nearby GeckoTerminal snapshot showed roughly $476,000 in liquidity and about $1.0 million in 24-hour volume. The structure shows a tokenized equity being used not merely as a stock-linked asset or USDC LP component, but as the quote asset for another speculative token.

Market Watch · 2026-09-02

Aerodrome Tokenized-Stock LP Rewards Re-Expand Across AAPLc, METAc and GOOGLc

Displayed yields on Aerodrome Slipstream tokenized-stock/USDC pools expanded again. AAPLc showed 375.31% APY with about $1.16 million TVL, METAc 657.17% with about $946,000 TVL and GOOGLc 349.84% with about $1.32 million TVL, with rewards accounting for most of the increase.

Market Watch · 2026-09-02

Aerodrome NVDAc LP Rebounds Above 300% APY After a Sharp Drop

Aerodrome Slipstream USDC-NVDAc on Base rebounded from roughly 58% APY in the prior patrol to above 300%. DeFi Terminal showed 351.08% and DefiLlama 314.43%, while TVL fell from about $2.0 million to roughly $1.65-$1.70 million.

Market Watch · 2026-09-02

Base Tokenized-Stock Incentives Expand from LP Rewards to Direct Cashback

Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base are now being incentivized through direct USDC cashback for purchases held through an epoch, without requiring an LP or vault. Merkl listed up to 5% cashback via 1inch and up to 2% through Reserve and Definitive across AAPLc, GOOGLc, NVDAc and METAc opportunities.
